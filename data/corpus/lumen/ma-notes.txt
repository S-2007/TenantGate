WHAT THIS IS
------------
Running notes on the proposed acquisition of Brightpath Payments, Inc. ("Target"), a payments facilitator based in Phoenix focused on home-services and trade-contractor merchants. Use the codename in all communications. Do not use the Target's name in email subject lines or calendar invites.


DEAL HEADLINE
-------------
  - Proposed purchase price: $112 million enterprise value, cash-free / debt-free.
  - Structure: 70% cash at close, 30% Lumen stock; plus up to $15M earnout tied to 2027 net revenue.
  - Financing: cash on hand plus a new $60M term facility (two lenders indicating interest).
  - LOI signed Aug 28; exclusivity through Nov 30.
  - Target close: Q1 2027, subject to bank partner approval and HSR review if required.


WHY WE WANT IT
--------------
1. About 9,600 active merchants, ~70% in trades and home services - a segment where our Flex Line has the best credit performance.
2. Their field-sales team (31 reps) in Arizona and Nevada, where we're weak.
3. A working integration with two popular field-service scheduling apps, which would take us 12+ months to build.
4. Cross-sell: our modeling says 18-22% of their merchants qualify for Lumen Capital in the first year.
