# Q2 FY2026 Quarterly Financial Report

Quarter ended June 30, 2026 · Prepared by FP&A (Benedikt Oyama, Director of FP&A) · Approved by Tobias Kwan, CFO
_Issued 2026-07-24 (management accounts, unaudited)_

---

## 1. Headline

Q2 revenue was $29.8 million, up 31% year over year and 6% over Q1. We beat the revenue plan by $0.6M, mostly from payments, while lending came in under plan because we tightened Sales-Based Advance underwriting in May. Adjusted EBITDA was negative $1.4 million, better than the plan of negative $2.2 million.

## 2. Revenue breakdown

| Line | Q2 FY26 | Q1 FY26 | Q2 FY25 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Payments (Lumen Pay net revenue) | $19.6M | $18.3M | $14.2M | +38% |
| Same-Day Payouts fees (pilot) | $0.4M | $0.1M | — | n/a |
| Lending (Lumen Capital: interest, fees, servicing) | $8.1M | $8.0M | $7.0M | +16% |
| Subscriptions (Growth plan) | $1.7M | $1.7M | $1.5M | +13% |
| **Total revenue** | **$29.8M** | **$28.1M** | **$22.7M** | **+31%** |

Payments revenue is shown net of interchange and network fees. Gross processing volume (GPV) was $1.62 billion in the quarter, up 34% YoY. Net take rate held at 1.21%.

## 3. Costs and margin

- **Gross margin:** 52.3% (Q1: 51.1%). Improvement came from the renegotiated Corvane processing contract, effective April 1.
- **Credit losses (provision):** $1.6M, or 6.8% annualized of average on-balance-sheet loans. Up from 6.1% in Q1, driven by restaurants in the Sales-Based Advance book.
- **Operating expenses:** $17.0M.
    - R&D: $7.9M
    - Sales & marketing: $4.6M
    - G&A: $4.5M (includes $0.6M of one-off costs for the security incident response and merchant notifications in May)

## 5. Balance sheet & liquidity

- Cash and equivalents: $86.3M at June 30 (Mar 31: $91.0M).
- Loan book (on our balance sheet portion, after bank partner participation): $96.2M outstanding.
- Warehouse facility drawn: $58.0M of $100M.
- Monthly net burn averaged $1.6M. At current burn, runway is well over 36 months, but the planned acquisition would use a meaningful part of cash — see the separate Corp Dev notes.

## 6. KPIs

* Active merchants: 41,200 (+2,100 in the quarter)
* Merchants on Growth plan: 14,500
* Loans originated: 2,940 totalling $71.5M
* 30+ day delinquency: 4.9% (Q1: 4.4%)
* Merchant churn (monthly, logo): 1.3%

## 7. Outlook for Q3

We expect Q3 revenue of $31.0M–$31.8M, helped by the full launch of Same-Day Payouts in September. Lending growth will stay muted while the Sales-Based Advance product is narrowed. Opex will rise temporarily due to one-time restructuring costs (details shared separately with the board). Full-year FY2026 revenue guidance is unchanged at $120M–$124M.

## 8. Risks to watch

1. Credit losses in the restaurant segment if consumer spending softens.
2. Chargeback ratio on a small number of high-volume contractors.
3. Interest-rate changes affecting warehouse facility cost (SOFR + 4.25%).
4. Concentration: top 10 partnership accounts are ~14% of payments revenue.
