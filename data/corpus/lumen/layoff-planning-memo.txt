1. Summary

The Q3 restructuring codename is PROJECT-BLUEHERON-LUMEN. Please use only the codename in calendar invites, document titles and Slack.

The plan reduces headcount by 38 roles, which is about 6.3% of our current 603 employees. The goal is to bring operating expenses in line with the revised FY2026 plan after the slowdown in Sales-Based Advance originations and the decision to consolidate Merchant Support tooling. Target notification date is Tuesday, September 29, 2026.


2. Where the reductions fall

   Team                                   Roles   Notes
   -----------------------------------    -----   ----------------------------------
   Merchant Support (Austin)                14    after chatbot + new help center rollout
   Lending Operations - SBA advances        9     product line being narrowed
   Sales (outbound SMB)                     6     moving to partner-led channel
   Marketing                                4     brand team consolidated
   Engineering (Lumen Hub legacy web)       3     legacy app sunset
   G&A (facilities, recruiting coord.)      2
                                          ----
   Total                                    38

No reductions are planned in Risk & Fraud, Compliance, Security or Payments Platform. We are still hiring in Denver for underwriting and fraud analytics, which may raise questions; managers need a clear answer (talking points to follow from Comms).


3. Severance and support (proposed)

  - Base severance: 8 weeks of base pay, plus 1 additional week per full year of service, capped at 20 weeks total.
  - COBRA premiums paid by Lumen for 3 months.
  - Equity: accelerate vesting for the next quarterly tranche for anyone affected.
  - Outplacement services through Ridgeline Careers for 90 days.
  - Unused PTO paid out regardless of state.
  - Laptops may be kept after IT wipes them (approx. cost $18,000 total).

Estimated one-time cost: $2.45 million (severance $1.9M, COBRA $310K, outplacement $95K, other $145K). Annualized savings: approximately $4.1 million.


4. Legal considerations (S. Lindqvist to confirm)

  - WARN Act: the reduction is spread across two sites, which keeps us below the federal threshold, and Texas has no state mini-WARN. We will still provide at least 2 weeks' notice period pay in lieu.
  - Adverse impact analysis by age, gender and race must be done before final selection. Lena Hoang to run with outside counsel by August 21.
  - Two employees in scope are currently on parental leave and one on approved medical leave; Legal to review individually. No one on protected leave will be notified during their leave.
  - Visa holders: one H-1B employee in the Engineering list - immigration counsel to advise on the 60-day grace period.
