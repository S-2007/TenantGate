## How to read this

- Bands are **base salary only**, annual, in USD. Equity and bonus targets are in the separate Total Rewards workbook.
- Bands are set at the 50th–75th percentile of our fintech peer group using the Q4 2025 survey data (Pave + Radford).
- Austin and Denver are both "Tier 2" geo. Fully remote employees in Tier 1 metros (SF Bay Area, NYC, Seattle) get a +8% geo adjustment applied on top of the band.
- New-hire offers above the band midpoint need VP People approval. Anything above the band max needs CFO approval and is rare.

## Notes for managers

1. **Don't share bands with candidates** beyond the posted range in the job ad. The posted range is usually the bottom 80% of the band.
2. Compa-ratio below 0.90 for a "Strong Performer" or above gets flagged for an adjustment in the March cycle.
3. Engineers moving L4 → L5 typically see a 10–14% increase; check you have budget headroom with your HRBP first.
4. Our 2026 merit budget is 3.5% of base payroll, plus a 0.5% pool for equity adjustments (people below band minimum).
