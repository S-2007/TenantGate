# Key Customer Contract Terms — Enterprise / Partnership Accounts

Maintained by: Deal Desk (Nadia Petrosyan) with Legal
Last updated: Sept 30 2026

This is a summary of non-standard commercial terms for our largest partnership accounts. The signed agreements in Ironclad are the source of truth; if this summary disagrees with a contract, the contract wins. Standard pricing for everyone else is on the public website.

---

## 1. Ridgeway Hardware Co-op

**Who they are:** a member-owned purchasing cooperative of 312 independent hardware and farm-supply stores across Texas, Oklahoma, Kansas and New Mexico. Each member store is its own Lumen merchant, but pricing is negotiated centrally by the co-op.

**Agreement:** Master Merchant Services Agreement + Lending Referral Addendum, signed March 3, 2026. Initial term 36 months, auto-renews for 12-month periods unless either party gives 90 days' notice.

**Pricing:**

- Ridgeway Hardware Co-op receives a 22% discount off standard Lumen Pay processing rates for all member stores.
- In practice: in-person card rate of 2.03% + 8¢ (vs. 2.6% + 10¢ standard); online 2.26% + 23¢.
- Growth plan monthly fee waived for all member stores.
- Same-Day Payouts at 0.75% instead of 1%.

**Service levels:**

- Ridgeway Hardware Co-op has a 99.95% uptime SLA on card authorization, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced 5 days in advance.
- SLA credits: if uptime falls below 99.95%, credit of 10% of that month's processing fees for affected stores; below 99.5%, 25%; below 99.0%, 50%. Credits are capped at 50% of the month's fees and are the co-op's sole remedy for downtime.
- Dedicated support line with a 2-minute answer target, 7 a.m. – 9 p.m. CT.
- Named Partner Success Manager: Trevor Ashby.

**Volume commitment:** the co-op commits to $410 million of annual processing volume across members. If volume falls below $330 million in any contract year, the discount steps down to 15% for the following year.

**Lending:** Lumen pays the co-op a referral fee of 1.25% of funded Flex Line and Term Loan amounts for member stores, paid quarterly.

**Notes:** they've asked twice about a ACH rate below 0.5%. Finance said no in July. Expect it again at the annual review in March 2027.

## 2. Alamo Fresh Markets (regional grocery, 46 stores)

- 11% discount off standard card rates.
- 99.9% uptime SLA (same as public target), credit 5% of monthly fees below that.
- 24-month term, renewal date January 2027. Account health: yellow — they've had two Beacon false-positive holds this year and complained.

## 3. Copperline Franchise Group (quick-service restaurants, 128 locations)

- Interchange-plus pricing: interchange + 0.35% + 6¢.
- Lumen Capital: exclusive financing partner for franchisee remodels; $25M total program limit.
- 99.9% uptime SLA, credits per standard schedule.
- Requires 60 days' notice before any change to the reader hardware.

## 4. Standard SLA credit schedule (reference)

| Monthly uptime | Credit (% of monthly processing fees) |
|---|---|
| < 99.9% | 5% |
| < 99.5% | 10% |
| < 99.0% | 25% |

Non-standard SLAs (like Ridgeway's) need sign-off from the CTO and CFO, because they create a real financial exposure. Q2 2026 actual authorization uptime was 99.97%, so no credits were triggered for any account.

## Approval matrix for new non-standard deals

1. Discount up to 10%: Deal Desk.
2. Discount 10–20%: VP Sales + Finance (FP&A).
3. Discount above 20% or any SLA above 99.9%: CFO Tobias Kwan and CTO Ravi Menon.
4. Anything with uncapped liability: General Counsel — and the answer is almost always no.
