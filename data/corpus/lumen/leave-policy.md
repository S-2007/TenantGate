# Leave & Time Off Policy

**Policy owner:** People Operations · **Applies to:** all U.S. employees of Lumen Finance, Inc.
**Effective:** January 1, 2026 · Last updated: 2026-01-09

---

## 1. Purpose

Rest is part of doing good work. This policy explains how much paid time off you get, how to request it, and what other types of leave are available.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues each pay period (semi-monthly), so you earn 0.833 days per pay period. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro-rata based on scheduled hours.

- PTO can be used for vacation, personal days, appointments or anything else.
- You can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Anything above 5 days is forfeited on January 1 unless state law says otherwise.
- New hires can borrow up to 3 days of PTO before they have accrued it, with manager approval.
- Request PTO in Workday at least **two weeks** in advance for anything longer than 3 consecutive days.

During the month-end close (last 3 business days of each month and first 3 of the next), Finance and Treasury teams should avoid scheduling PTO unless agreed with their manager. Similar freeze windows apply to Payments Engineering during the November peak period (week of Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday).

## 3. Sick Leave

Every employee gets 8 sick days per year, available from the start of the year (pro-rated for new hires). Sick days don't carry over.

Use sick leave for your own illness or injury, medical appointments, or caring for a sick family member. You don't need a doctor's note unless you're out for more than 3 consecutive working days. Where state or local law requires more generous sick leave (for example in Denver), the local rules apply.

## 4. Company Holidays

Lumen observes 11 paid holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. On-call staff who work a holiday get a substitute day off within 60 days.

## 5. Parental Leave

Lumen offers 14 weeks paid parental leave to any employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption, or foster placement, regardless of gender. You're eligible after 90 days of employment.

* Leave can be taken in one continuous block or in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or placement.
* Pay is 100% of base salary for the full period.
* Birthing parents can also use short-term disability (typically 6–8 weeks), which runs **concurrently** with parental leave, not in addition to it.
* Let your manager and People Ops know at least 30 days before your expected leave start, where possible.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or in-law), and up to 2 paid days for other relatives or close friends.

## 7. Other Leave

| Type | Paid? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jury duty | Yes, up to 10 days | Bring the summons to your manager |
| Voting | Yes, up to 2 hours | Election days only |
| Military leave | Differential pay for up to 30 days | per USERRA |
| FMLA | Unpaid (can use PTO) | up to 12 weeks, after 12 months of employment |
| Volunteer days | Yes, 2 days per year | must be a registered nonprofit |

## 8. How to Request

1. Submit the request in Workday under Time Off.
2. Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days.
3. Put it on your calendar and set an out-of-office reply that says who to contact.

If you're on an on-call rotation, find a swap and update PagerDuty **before** you submit your PTO request.
