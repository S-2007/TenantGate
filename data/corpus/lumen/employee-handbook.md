Version 4.2 — last updated 03/17/2026

## 1. Welcome

Lumen Finance moves money for small businesses — about 41,000 of them. Our merchants trust us with their payroll money, their rent money and, through Lumen Capital, their growth plans. That trust is the whole business. Everything in this handbook flows from it.

We are about 600 people, mostly in Austin (HQ, East 6th Street) and Denver (lending + risk hub, opened Feb 2026), plus a group of fully remote engineers approved under the remote work policy.

## 3. Working hours and attendance

Core collaboration hours are **10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Central Time**, Monday through Friday. Outside of core hours, arrange your day with your manager. Merchant Support and the on-call rotations have scheduled shifts; your manager will share your schedule in Deputy.

If you're going to be out unexpectedly (sick, car broke down, kid's school called), message your manager before your start time and set your Slack status. Planned time off goes in Workday — see the Leave Policy for allowances and how to request.

Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked in Workday, including any time spent answering Slack after hours. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager, but you will always be paid for hours you actually worked.

## 4. Pay and payroll

- Payroll runs **semi-monthly** on the 15th and the last business day of the month, via direct deposit.
- Pay stubs and W-2s are in Workday under Pay.
- Merit increases are reviewed once a year in the March cycle; promotions can happen in March or September.
- Equity grants vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Carta holds your grant documents.

If you think your paycheck is wrong, email payroll@lumenfinance.example within the same pay period if possible. Don't wait — it's much easier to fix quickly.

## 5. Benefits (overview)

Full-time employees (30+ hours/week) are eligible for benefits from their first day. The benefits portal (Benefitfocus) has plan documents and costs. Headlines:

* Medical — two plans, a PPO and a high-deductible plan with an HSA. Lumen contributes $750/yr to the HSA for individual coverage.
* Vision and basic life insurance (1x salary) are fully paid by Lumen.
* Employee Assistance Program — free, confidential counseling (6 sessions per issue) through Lantern EAP.
* Learning stipend: $1,200 per calendar year. Submit through Navan like any other expense.
* Commuter benefit: pre-tax transit passes via the benefits portal.

Open enrollment is every November. Life events (marriage, a new child, loss of other coverage) let you change elections within 30 days.

## 6. Code of conduct

We expect everyone to:

1. Treat colleagues, merchants and partners with respect.
2. Be honest — in what you say, what you record, and what you report.
3. Protect merchant and cardholder data as if it were your own bank account.
4. Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose them when they come up.
5. Follow the law, our bank partner's requirements, and card network rules.

Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. That includes jokes, slurs, unwanted contact, and retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

### 6.1 Conflicts of interest

Disclose in the Workday "Conflict of Interest" form if you (or a close family member) hold more than 1% of a competitor, supplier or merchant; take an outside job; sit on a board; or are in a relationship with someone in your reporting line. Disclosure isn't an admission of anything — it lets us manage the situation.

### 6.2 Gifts

You may accept gifts or meals from vendors worth up to **$100 per vendor per year**. Anything above that, or any gift of cash or gift cards, must be declined or reported to Legal. Never accept anything from a merchant whose loan application, dispute or account review you are working on.

### 6.3 Personal trading and confidential information

Because we work with bank partners and occasionally evaluate other companies, you may learn non-public information. Do not trade securities based on it and don't share it outside the people who need it. Confidential documents are labeled; if something says CONFIDENTIAL, treat it that way even if a link to it was shared with you by mistake.

## 7. Speaking up

You can raise a concern with your manager, People Ops, Legal, or anonymously through the **Lumen Ethics Line** (run by a third party, 24/7, web or phone). Compliance concerns — suspected money laundering, a merchant you think is fraudulent, a control that isn't working — can also go straight to the BSA Officer. We investigate every report and we do not retaliate. Ever.

## 8. Workplace basics (Austin and Denver)

- Offices are open 7 a.m. – 8 p.m. on weekdays; your badge works after hours if your manager requests it.
- Visitors must be registered in Envoy and escorted at all times. No visitors in the Risk Operations room in Denver.
- Clean desk: lock your screen when you step away and don't leave printed merchant data on your desk. Shred bins are by every printer.
- Snacks and coffee are free. The Austin kitchen hosts lunch on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## 9. Performance and growth

Performance reviews happen twice a year (March and September). Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback from 2–4 colleagues, and a manager write-up. Ratings use a four-point scale: **Needs Improvement, Solid Contributor, Strong Performer, Exceptional**. Ratings feed into the March merit cycle.

Managers hold 1:1s at least every two weeks. If you haven't had one in a month, say so — it's on them, not you.

Internal transfers are encouraged after 12 months in a role. Talk to your manager first, then apply on the internal jobs board.

## 10. Leaving Lumen

We ask for two weeks' notice where possible. Your last paycheck includes any accrued, unused PTO as required by the state where you work. Return your laptop, badge and Lumen Tap test devices to IT by your last day; IT will send a prepaid box if you're remote. Your access to systems ends at 5 p.m. local time on your final day.
