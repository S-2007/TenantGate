# Frequently Asked Questions

*Lumen Finance help center - general questions. Last reviewed 14 August 2026.*

---

## Account & sign-up

**Who can open a Lumen account?**
Any business registered in the United States, including sole proprietors, LLCs, partnerships and corporations. We can't currently support businesses in cannabis, firearms sales, adult entertainment, cryptocurrency exchanges or online gambling.

**What do I need to sign up?**
Your legal business name, EIN (or SSN if you are a sole proprietor), a U.S. business bank account, and a photo of a government-issued ID for each owner with 25% or more ownership. We're required to collect this under federal "know your customer" rules.

**How long does approval take?**
Most accounts are approved instantly. If we need to review documents, our onboarding team will usually finish within one business day.

## Payments

**How much does Lumen Pay cost?**
In-person card payments cost 2.6% + 10¢. Online and keyed-in card payments cost 2.9% + 30¢. ACH bank payments cost 0.8% with a $5 cap. There is no setup fee, and the Starter plan has no monthly fee. The Growth plan is $39 per month and adds Same-Day Payouts, advanced reporting and up to 25 team logins.

**When do I get my money?**
By default, card sales settle to your bank account on the next business day. If you are on the Growth plan you can turn on Same-Day Payouts: sales captured before 2:00 p.m. Central Time arrive by 6:00 p.m. Central Time the same business day, for a 1% fee.

**Why is my payout on hold?**
Occasionally a payout is held for review if Beacon, our fraud system, sees something unusual, such as a sudden spike in refunds or very large transactions on a new account. Holds are usually cleared within 48 hours. You will get an email explaining what we need.

**What happens with chargebacks?**
When a cardholder disputes a charge, we notify you in Lumen Hub and by email. You'll have 7 days to upload evidence (receipts, delivery confirmation, signed invoices). We submit it to the card network for you. There's a $15 fee per chargeback, refunded if you win.

## Lending (Lumen Capital)

**Will checking my offer affect my credit score?**
No. Seeing your pre-qualified offer uses a soft credit inquiry. A hard inquiry only happens if you accept an offer and submit a full application.

**How much can I borrow?**
Flex Lines of Credit go up to $150,000 and Term Loans up to $250,000. Your offer depends on your sales history, time in business (at least 9 months) and credit profile.

**Who actually makes the loan?**
Loans are issued by Harbor Plains Bank, Member FDIC, and serviced by Lumen Finance. You make payments to Lumen.

**Can I pay early?**
Yes. There are no prepayment penalties on Flex Lines or Term Loans. For Sales-Based Advances the fixed fee is set at the start, so paying early does not reduce it.

## Security

**Is my customers' card data safe?**
Lumen is a PCI DSS Level 1 service provider. Card numbers are encrypted at the reader and tokenized; we never store full card numbers in Lumen Hub, and you never have to.

**Does Lumen ever ask for my password?**
Never. Lumen staff will never ask for your password or one-time login codes by phone, text or email. If someone does, report it to security@lumenfinance.example.

## Support
