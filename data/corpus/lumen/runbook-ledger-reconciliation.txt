WHAT THIS IS

Prism is Lumen's double-entry ledger. Every card sale, refund, payout, fee and loan disbursement is written to Prism as balanced journal entries. Each night at 02:00 CT the job "recon-nightly" compares Prism totals to:

  (a) the settlement files from Corvane and Tessaline (card processing),
  (b) the bank statement files (BAI2) from our operating and settlement accounts at Harbor Plains Bank, and
  (c) the payout file we sent the previous day.

If any account differs by more than $50.00, or if the count of unmatched items is above 25, the job raises a "recon break" alert in PagerDuty (service: ledger-recon). Breaks must be cleared before the 09:00 CT Treasury funding decision, because Treasury uses Prism balances to decide how much to pre-fund the settlement account.

WHEN YOU GET PAGED

1. Ack the page. Recon breaks are usually SEV3. Upgrade to SEV2 if the break is more than $250,000, and to SEV1 if it looks like payouts will be wrong or late.
2. Open the recon report: Retool > Ledger > Recon Nightly > today's run.
3. Note the break type. The four common types are below.

COMMON BREAK TYPES

  TYPE A - Missing settlement file
     Symptom: entire processor column is blank.
     Cause: processor SFTP drop was late.
     Fix: check s3://lumen-settlement-inbound/<processor>/<date>/. If the file arrived after 02:00, re-run:
        lumenctl recon rerun --date YYYY-MM-DD --source corvane
     If no file by 06:00, contact the processor (contacts in 1Password "Payments-Vendors") and tell Treasury.

  TYPE B - Timing differences
     Symptom: small number of items, each matched the next day.
     Cause: transactions captured near midnight UTC land in different files.
     Fix: none needed if under $50,000 total and clears in the next run. Annotate the break in Retool as "timing" and resolve.

  TYPE C - Duplicate journal entries
     Symptom: Prism higher than bank by an exact multiple of a single payout or fee.
     Cause: retried writes from payout-service when the idempotency key was missing (see INC-2291).
     Fix: identify duplicates with the saved query "dupe-finder-v3". Reversal entries MUST follow the two-person rule: one engineer prepares the reversal in Retool, a second engineer approves. Never delete journal entries - Prism is append-only and deletes are blocked anyway.

  TYPE D - Unknown bank items
     Symptom: bank shows debits/credits with no Prism match.
     Cause: varies - bank fees, returned ACH (R01/R02), chargebacks debited directly.
     Fix: hand to Payments Ops (#payments-ops) with the list. Engineering does not book these manually.

DO NOT
  - do not edit the recon tolerance thresholds to make a break "go away"
  - do not run the payout job manually while a recon break is open, unless the Treasury on-call explicitly agrees in the incident channel
  - do not paste merchant bank account numbers into Slack. Use the Prism item ID.

CLOSING OUT

  - Re-run recon-nightly for the date and confirm "0 open breaks" or only annotated timing items.
  - Post the summary in #ledger-recon: date, break type, $ amount, fix, ticket.
  - If manual reversals were made, attach the Retool audit export to the Jira ticket. Internal Audit samples these monthly.
  - For SEV2+ follow the normal incident and postmortem process.
