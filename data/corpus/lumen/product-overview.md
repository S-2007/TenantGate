# Lumen Finance — Product Overview

_Last updated: 2026-09-02 · Owner: Product Marketing (Ines Carvalho)_

Lumen Finance builds payments and lending tools for small businesses. We are headquartered in Austin, Texas, and serve roughly 41,000 merchants across the United States, most of them independent retailers, restaurants, trade contractors and service shops with fewer than 50 employees.

Our view is simple: a small business should be able to take a payment, see the money, and get working capital without three different vendors and a fax machine. Everything below runs on a single merchant account and a single dashboard.

## Lumen Pay

Lumen Pay is our payment acceptance product.

- **In-person:** the Lumen Tap card reader (contactless, chip, and swipe) and Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android.
- **Online:** hosted checkout pages, payment links you can text or email, and a REST API for developers.
- **Invoicing:** send branded invoices with a "pay now" button; automatic reminders at 3, 7 and 14 days.
- **Pricing:** 2.6% + 10¢ for in-person card transactions and 2.9% + 30¢ for online card transactions. ACH payments are 0.8%, capped at $5 per transaction. No monthly fee on the Starter plan.

Funds settle to the merchant's bank account the next business day by default. Merchants on the Growth plan can turn on **Same-Day Payouts** for an additional 1% of the payout amount.

## Lumen Capital

Lumen Capital is our small-business lending line.

| Product | Amount | Term | How it is repaid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flex Line of Credit | $5,000 - $150,000 | revolving, 12-month draw period | weekly ACH debit |
| Term Loan | $10,000 - $250,000 | 6 to 24 months | fixed weekly or monthly payments |
| Sales-Based Advance | $2,000 - $80,000 | until repaid | a fixed % of daily Lumen Pay sales |

Lending decisions use the merchant's own Lumen Pay processing history, bank data (with permission) and standard credit bureau checks. Most applications get a decision in under 24 hours. Loans are issued by our bank partner, Harbor Plains Bank, Member FDIC, and serviced by Lumen Finance.

## Lumen Hub (the dashboard)

Lumen Hub is the web and mobile app where merchants run everything:

1. Real-time sales and payout tracking
2. Dispute and chargeback management, with evidence upload
3. Capital offers and loan balance
4. Team permissions (owner, manager, cashier roles)
5. Exports to QuickBooks and Xero

## Platform & trust

* PCI DSS Level 1 service provider
* SOC 2 Type II report available to customers under NDA
* 24/7 fraud monitoring powered by our in-house risk engine, **Beacon**
* Public uptime target of 99.9% for the payments API; real-time status at status.lumenfinance.example

## Who it is for

Lumen is designed for businesses processing between $50,000 and $10 million a year. Larger merchants and franchise groups can talk to our Partnerships team about custom pricing and dedicated support.

## Getting started

Sign-up takes about ten minutes. You will need your EIN or SSN (for sole proprietors), a business bank account, and a government ID for the business owner. Most accounts are approved instantly; some are reviewed by our onboarding team within one business day.
