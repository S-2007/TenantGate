New Hire Onboarding Guide
=========================

People Ops + IT · rev. July 2026 · owner: Camila Ruiz (Onboarding Program Lead)

Welcome to Lumen! This guide walks through your first 30 days. Your manager and your onboarding buddy have a copy too, so nobody should be guessing.

## Before day one

- Your laptop (MacBook Pro for engineering, MacBook Air for everyone else) ships to your home address 5 business days before your start date. Don't open the sealed security bag until your Day 1 IT session.
- You'll get a personal email with a link to activate your **Okta** account. Set up Okta Verify on your phone — you'll need it for everything.
- Complete your I-9 section 1 and tax forms in Workday.

## Week 1 checklist

1. Finish **required training** in LearnUpon by Friday:
   - Information Security Awareness (45 min)
   - BSA/AML & Sanctions Basics (60 min) — required for *every* employee, not just Compliance
   - PCI DSS for Everyone (30 min)
   - Harassment Prevention (60 min, or 120 min for managers)
2. Read the Employee Handbook, the IT Security Policy, and the Remote Work Policy, then sign the acknowledgement in Workday.
3. Join Slack channels: #announcements, #ask-people, #ask-it, #incidents (read-only for most people), your team channel.
4. Set up direct deposit and benefits elections. You have **30 days** from your start date to enroll in benefits.
5. Book a 20-minute coffee chat with at least three people outside your team.

## Access requests

Access is role-based and requested through the **AccessHub** form in ServiceNow. Your manager approves; Security approves anything touching production, the Prism ledger, or merchant PII. Typical turnaround is 1–2 business days.

Please do not ask a teammate to "just share their login" while you wait. It's a fireable offense under the IT Security Policy and also, honestly, it makes our auditors cry.

Engineers: production access is not granted in your first two weeks. You'll pair with your buddy on deploys first, then complete the "Prod Readiness" checklist (includes shadowing one on-call shift).

## Weeks 2–4

* Shadow a Merchant Support shift (2 hours). Everyone does this — CFO included. Hearing a bakery owner explain a chargeback is the best training we have.
* Attend the monthly All Hands (first Thursday, 11 a.m. CT).
* Agree your first-quarter goals with your manager and enter them in Lattice.
* Engineers: ship something to production by day 30, even if it's a small fix.

## Who to ask

- **Benefits, payroll, PTO:** #ask-people or people@lumenfinance.example
- **Laptop, accounts, access:** #ask-it or the ServiceNow portal
- **"Is this a security problem?":** #security-help or security@lumenfinance.example — when in doubt, report it
- **Expenses:** #ask-finance; the Expense Policy is on the intranet under Finance

## 30-day check-in

At day 30 you and your manager will do a short check-in using the template in Lattice: what's going well, what's confusing, what you need. People Ops also sends a 5-question anonymous survey about your onboarding — please fill it in, we really do change things based on it. (The Day 1 schedule moved lunch earlier last quarter because of survey feedback.)
