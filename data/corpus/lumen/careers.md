CAREERS AT LUMEN FINANCE
========================

last updated Oct 6 2026

We're about 600 people building the financial tools we wish our parents' corner stores had. Most of us work out of our Austin headquarters on East 6th Street, and we opened a second hub in Denver this year for our lending and risk teams.

## Why people join

Small businesses run on thin margins. When a payout lands a day late, or a loan application sits for three weeks, real people feel it. Our teams ship things that change those numbers: last year we cut average loan decision time from 4 days to under 24 hours, and moved over 30% of merchants onto next-day or same-day settlement.

## How hiring works

1. **Apply online.** A real person on our talent team reads every application, usually within 5 business days.
2. **Recruiter call** (30 min) — background, what you want next, logistics.
3. **Hiring manager conversation** (45 min).
4. **Skills interview.** For engineers this is a paired coding session on a realistic problem, not a puzzle. For other roles it may be a case study or a short written exercise.
5. **Team loop** — 2 to 3 conversations with future teammates.
6. **Offer.** We aim to get you an answer within a week of your final interview.

We do not ask for your salary history. Every posting includes a pay range.

## Our values, in plain words

* **Merchants first.** If it doesn't help a business owner, ask why we're doing it.
* **Own the outcome.** Ship it, watch it, fix it.
* **Say the hard thing early.** Bad news doesn't age well.
* **Money is trust.** We move other people's money, so we are careful and we are honest.
