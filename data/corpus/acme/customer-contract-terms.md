# Key Customer Contract Terms Summary - Halvorsen Packaging

_Deal desk summary prepared by Carla Mendes (Revenue Operations), reviewed by Finance. Last updated 2026-07-08._

This summary covers the master agreement signed with Halvorsen Packaging, our largest customer by FleetSight subscription count. Use it for revenue recognition, renewals planning, and SLA credit calculations. The signed agreement in the contract repository is the controlling document if anything here differs.

## Parties and term

- **Customer:** Halvorsen Packaging (corrugated and folding-carton packaging, 11 plants in the US Midwest and Southeast)
- **Agreement:** Master Purchase and Services Agreement MPSA-2026-031, plus Order Forms 1-3
- **Effective date:** March 1, 2026
- **Initial term:** 3 years, auto-renewing for 1-year terms unless either party gives 90 days' notice

## Scope

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ARC-12 palletizing cells | 24 | delivered in 3 waves through Q1 2027 |
| ARC-6 machine tending cells | 10 | |
| Tern AMR | 18 | end-of-line material movement |
| FleetSight Enterprise subscription | all 52 robots + 61 existing Acme robots | per-robot annual fee |
| AcmeCare Plus | all new units | 60-month coverage |

## Pricing

Halvorsen Packaging receives an 18% discount off list price on all hardware, FleetSight subscriptions and AcmeCare Plus purchased under this agreement during the initial term. The discount does not apply to field integration labor, which is billed at the standard time-and-materials rate card, or to spare parts outside AcmeCare coverage.

- Total contract value (3 years): approx. $14.8M after discount
- Payment terms: Net 45 (our standard is Net 30; approved by CFO as part of the deal)
- Price protection: list prices for the covered products are frozen for the initial term. Any price increase after renewal is capped at 4% per year.

## FleetSight service level agreement

Acme commits to 99.5% uptime for FleetSight, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance (maximum 6 hours per month).

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, Halvorsen receives SLA credits against the next FleetSight invoice:

| Monthly uptime | Credit (% of monthly FleetSight fee) |
|---|---|
| 99.0% to below 99.5% | 10% |
| 98.0% to below 99.0% | 25% |
| below 98.0% | 50% |

Credits are Halvorsen's sole remedy for FleetSight availability, except that three consecutive months below 98.0% gives Halvorsen the right to terminate the FleetSight portion of the agreement without penalty. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the month.

## Hardware terms

- Delivery: ARC-12 cells within 18 weeks of each wave's order form. Late delivery over 4 weeks triggers a 1% price reduction per week on the affected cells, capped at 8%.
- Acceptance: each cell has a 30-day acceptance window after commissioning, against cycle-time and uptime criteria in Order Form 2.
- Warranty: standard plus AcmeCare Plus as above.

## Security and data

- Halvorsen telemetry stays in US regions.
- Security incident notification: within 48 hours of confirmation of any incident affecting Halvorsen data. (Note: Halvorsen was **not** among the customers affected in April; Legal confirmed.)
- Annual right to audit our FleetSight security controls, or accept our SOC 2 Type II report in lieu.

## Other notable clauses

- Most-favored-customer clause: none. We refused this. Do not agree to one in renewal discussions without CFO approval.
- Liability cap: 12 months of fees paid, excluding confidentiality and indemnification breaches.
- Halvorsen may publicly reference Acme as a supplier; Acme needs written consent to name Halvorsen in marketing.

## Renewal notes

Account owner Brian Kasprzak expects to propose a second phase (Kite cobots for case packing, approx. 30 units) in mid-2027. Any additional discount beyond 18% needs Deal Desk and CFO approval.
