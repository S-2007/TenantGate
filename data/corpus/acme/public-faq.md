# Frequently Asked Questions

*Acme Robotics customer & public FAQ - updated 05/14/2026*

### Ordering and delivery

**Q: How long does it take to get a robot after I order?**
A: Standard lead time for ARC-6 and ARC-12 arms is 10 to 14 weeks from purchase order. ARC-20 lead time is currently 16 to 20 weeks. Kite cobots and Tern AMRs usually ship within 8 weeks. Your sales engineer can confirm a delivery date when you place the order.

**Q: Do you sell directly or through distributors?**
A: Both. We sell directly in the US and work with certified integration partners who can design and install a complete cell. If you already have an integrator you trust, we're happy to work with them.

**Q: Can I visit a demo cell before buying?**
A: Yes. Our Pittsburgh headquarters has a demo floor with working ARC, Kite and Tern cells. Book a visit through your sales engineer. We can also run a remote video demo with your parts if you ship us samples.

### Warranty and service

**Q: What warranty comes with an Acme robot?**
A: All Acme hardware comes with a standard 24-month warranty covering parts and labor from the date of commissioning. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship; it does not cover crash damage, misuse, or wear items such as cables in the dress pack.

**Q: Can I extend the warranty?**
A: Yes. AcmeCare Plus extends coverage to 60 months total and adds an annual preventive maintenance visit by a field engineer. AcmeCare Plus must be purchased before the standard warranty ends.

**Q: What are your support hours?**
A: Phone support is staffed Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern. Customers on AcmeCare Plus also get 24/7 emergency phone support for line-down situations.

**Q: How fast do spare parts ship?**
A: Common spare parts ship next business day within the continental US if ordered before 3:00 p.m. Eastern.

### Software

**Q: Is FleetSight required?**
A: No. Robots run fine without a network connection. FleetSight is included free for the first year for connected robots and after that is a subscription per robot. Air-gapped sites can still receive ArcOS firmware by USB.

**Q: Where is FleetSight data stored?**
A: FleetSight runs in US data centers operated by a major cloud provider. Telemetry is encrypted in transit and at rest. Customers can request an export or deletion of their data at any time.

**Q: Does CellForge run on Mac?**
A: Not natively. CellForge requires Windows 10 or 11. Some customers run it in a virtual machine, but we don't officially support that setup.

### Safety

**Q: Can Kite work next to people without a fence?**
A: Kite is designed for collaborative applications, but whether a specific cell can run without guarding depends on a risk assessment of the whole application, including the tool and the part. A sharp gripper on a cobot is still a sharp gripper. Our Field Integration team or your integrator should complete a risk assessment before any collaborative deployment.

**Q: Are your robots certified?**
A: ARC and Kite controllers are designed to meet the applicable international robot safety standards, and each robot ships with a declaration of conformity. Ask your sales engineer for the documentation package.
