New Hire Onboarding Guide
-------------------------

*People Ops + IT | last updated Feb 2026*

Welcome to Acme! This guide covers your first two weeks. Your manager and an onboarding buddy will walk you through most of it, but keep this handy - people forget things on day one, and that's fine.

## Before your first day

- You'll get an email from Workday about a week before you start. Finish the I-9 and tax forms there. Bring your I-9 documents (originals, not photocopies) on day one.
- Your manager will tell you whether you start at the Strip District HQ (2100 Smallman St) or the Mon Valley site. Field hires usually spend their first week at HQ.
- Wear closed-toe shoes. Even if you're in Finance, you'll be getting a floor tour.

## Day 1

**8:30 a.m.** - Check in at the HQ front desk. Security will take your photo and issue a blue employee badge.

**9:00 a.m.** - New hire orientation with People Ops in Training Room A (about two hours). Benefits, payroll, the handbook, and a lot of coffee.

**11:00 a.m.** - IT setup. Pick up your laptop at the IT Depot (room 3B, third floor) on Day 1. You must enroll in Okta Verify for multi-factor authentication before the end of your first day, because you won't be able to reach Slack, Jira or Workday without it after that. IT will help you set your first password and enroll your phone.

**12:00 p.m.** - Lunch with your team (on us).

**Afternoon** - Time with your manager: goals for your first 30/60/90 days, introductions, and access requests.

## Week 1 checklist

- [ ] Benefits enrollment in Workday (you have 30 days, but don't leave it to day 29)
- [ ] Sign the Acceptable Use and Confidentiality acknowledgments in Workday
- [ ] Complete the Security Awareness module in the learning portal
- [ ] Sign up for the next Safety 101 session (Tuesdays). Required before you can go on the floor unescorted.
- [ ] Join Slack channels: #general, #ask-people, #it-help, your team channel, and #wins if you like good news
- [ ] Set up your Jira account and ask your manager which boards to watch
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy at least twice

## Week 2

By week two most people are doing real work. Engineers usually ship their first change (often a docs fix or a small bug) by the end of week two. Assembly and test hires work alongside a certified trainer on the line and earn station sign-offs one at a time.

Some extra steps by team:

| Team | Extra onboarding |
|------|------------------|
| Software (FleetSight, CellForge) | Request GitHub org access, AWS SSO (read-only to start), and a CellForge dev license |
| Robotics & Controls | ArcOS build environment setup, lab bench assignment, ESD training |
| Field Integration | Travel profile in Navan, corporate card application, Field Safety course |
| Manufacturing | Kronos timekeeping badge enrollment, station training plan with your supervisor |
| Finance / People | NetSuite or Workday admin access as approved by your manager |

## Who to ask

* IT problems: #it-help or ext. 4357 (HELP)
* Payroll / benefits: #ask-people or people@acmerobotics.example
* Floor safety: the EHS team, or any supervisor in a yellow vest
* Anything else: your buddy. That's literally what they're for.

## Robotics 101

If you aren't an engineer, we strongly recomend Robotics 101: a two-hour, hands-on session where you'll jog an ARC-6 with the teach pendant and program a simple pick and place. It runs on the first Thursday of every month. People from Finance and Sales consistently say it's the most useful thing they did in their first month.

## 30-day check-in

At day 30 People Ops will send a short survey, and your manager will hold a check-in. Be honest. If something about onboarding was confusing, we want to fix it for the next person.
