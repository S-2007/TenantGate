# ACME ROBOTICS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Version 7.2 - Last updated: 12 Jan 2026
Owner: People Operations (Janelle Brooks, VP People)

> This handbook applies to all Acme Robotics employees in the United States, including the Strip District headquarters, the Mon Valley Assembly Center, and remote field staff. Where a specific policy (leave, expenses, remote work, IT security) exists as its own document, that document wins if the two ever disagree.

---

## 1. Welcome

Acme was founded in 2009 in a rented garage in Lawrenceville by three engineers who were tired of robots that needed a specialist to reprogram. We still believe the best automation is the kind a plant's own people can run. Today we are about 850 people, and most of what you will read here exists because somebody asked a reasonable question and we wrote the answer down.

Our values are short on purpose:

1. **Safety first, always.** Nobody is ever too busy to stop a line.
2. **Build it so a technician can fix it.**
3. **Say what you know, and say what you don't.**
4. **Own the outcome, not just the task.**

## 2. Working hours

Standard office hours are 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern. Office-based employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Eastern so meetings can be scheduled without endless calendar tetris.

Manufacturing runs two shifts at headquarters (6:00 a.m. - 2:30 p.m. and 2:30 p.m. - 11:00 p.m.). Shift assignments, overtime and shift swaps are handled by your production supervisor through the Kronos timekeeping system. Non-exempt employees must clock in and out for every shift and for unpaid meal breaks. Working "off the clock" is never allowed, even if you offer.

Field Integration engineers work customer schedules, which sometimes means nights and weekends during a commissioning. Comp time for field work is arranged with your manager.

## 3. Safety on the production floor

Everyone, including office staff, interns, and visitors with an escort, follows the Robot Cell Safety Standard when on the production or demo floor.

New employees must complete Safety 101 within their first 30 days, and nobody may enter the production floor unescorted until Safety 101 is complete. Safety 101 is a half-day course run by the EHS team every Tuesday morning in Training Room B.

Other basics:

- Safety glasses and safety-toe shoes are required past the yellow line. The company provides both.
- Never enter a guarded robot cell without applying your own personal lock (lockout/tagout).
- Report every injury, near miss, or unsafe condition in the EHS portal the same day. Near-miss reports are not discipline; they are how we find problems before someone gets hurt.
- Anyone can push an E-stop or stop a line for a safety concern. No one will be disciplined for a good-faith stop.

## 4. Equal opportunity and respectful workplace

Acme does not tolerate discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic. This applies to coworkers, managers, customers, vendors, and visitors.

Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this handbook and is treated as seriously as the original conduct.

## 5. Pay and payroll

Employees are paid every other Friday through direct deposit. Pay stubs and W-2s are available in Workday. If you believe your pay is wrong, contact payroll@acmerobotics.example within one pay period so it can be corrected on the next cycle.

Overtime for non-exempt employees is paid at 1.5x for hours over 40 in a workweek. Overtime must be approved in advance by your supervisor except in a genuine emergency (for example, a line-down situation at a customer).

## 6. Benefits overview

Full-time employees are eligible for medical, dental and vision coverage on the first day of employment. Enrollment must be completed in the Workday benefits portal within 30 days of your start date, or you will need to wait for open enrollment in November unless you have a qualifying life event.

Other benefits:

- 401(k) with a company match of 100% on the first 4% of pay you contribute, vested immediately
- Basic life and AD&D insurance at 1x annual salary, paid by Acme
- Short- and long-term disability insurance
- An Employee Assistance Program (EAP) with free confidential counseling sessions
- Annual training budget of $1,500 per employee for courses, certifications and conferences, approved by your manager

Time off, sick leave and parental leave are covered in the Leave Policy. Travel and reimbursement are in the Expense Policy.

## 7. Performance and growth

We run performance reviews twice a year, in March and September. Reviews include a self-assessment, manager assessment, and optional peer feedback. Ratings are: Below Expectations, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, and Outstanding.

Promotions are considered at the September cycle. Your manager should talk with you about where you are against the next level at least once a year - if that hasn't happened, ask.

## 8. Conduct

We expect people to act like adults. A few things deserve to be explicit:

- Do not work under the influence of alcohol, cannabis or any impairing substance. On the production floor this is a zero-tolerance rule.
- Do not share confidential information about customers, pricing, unreleased products, or other employees outside the people who need it.
- Conflicts of interest (for example, owning part of a supplier, or a close relative working for a competitor) must be disclosed to People Operations.
- Gifts from vendors or customers over $100 in value must be reported to your manager and Finance.

## 9. Raising concerns

You can raise any concern with your manager, your manager's manager, or People Operations. If you are not comfortable doing that, use the Acme Ethics Line, which is run by an independent provider and accepts anonymous reports 24 hours a day by phone or web. The Ethics Line number is posted on every break room bulletin board and on the intranet home page.

Concerns about harassment or discrimination can always go straight to People Operations without involving your manager.

## 10. Facilities and badges

Your ID badge opens the doors you need and nothing else. Wear it visibly above the waist at all times on site.

Badge colors tell people at a glance who is allowed where: blue badges are full-time employees, green badges are contractors, and orange badges are visitors who must be escorted at all times. If you see someone with an orange badge alone on the floor, politely walk them back to reception.

Lost badges must be reported to Security the same day so they can be disabled. A replacement costs nothing the first time.

The headquarters cafeteria is open 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on weekdays. The Mon Valley site has vending and a microwave break room; a full cafeteria is planned for 2027.

## 11. Leaving Acme

We ask for two weeks' notice for most roles and four weeks for people managers. You will return your laptop, badge, and any tools on your last day. Final pay is issued on the next regular payday. Unused PTO is paid out at separation where required by law and otherwise according to the Leave Policy.

## 12. Changes to this handbook

People Operations reviews the handbook every January. This handbook is not a contract of employment, and Acme may change these policies with notice. Questions go to people@acmerobotics.example or the #ask-people Slack channel.
