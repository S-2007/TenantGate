CAREERS AT ACME ROBOTICS
========================

last updated 2026-08-19

We build robots that make factory work safer and less repetitive. About 850 people work at Acme today, most of them at our headquarters in Pittsburgh's Strip District, with field engineers spread across Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Tennessee and the Carolinas.

If you like seeing your work leave the building on a pallet, you will probably like it here.

## Teams we hire for

* **Robotics & Controls Engineering** - motion planning, servo tuning, safety controllers, ArcOS firmware.
* **Software** - FleetSight (cloud, data pipelines, dashboards), CellForge (desktop simulation), internal tools.
* **Manufacturing & Assembly** - assemblers, test technicians, quality inspectors, production planners.
* **Field Integration** - the people who travel to customer plants and bring robot cells to life.
* **Customer Support** - phone and remote support for robots already in the field.
* **Business teams** - sales engineering, finance, people operations, supply chain, marketing.

## How to apply

All applications go through our careers portal at careers.acmerobotics.example. We do not accept resumes by email, and we never ask candidates to pay for anything during the hiring process. If someone claiming to be from Acme asks you for money or banking details before an offer, it is not us.

You can apply to up to three open roles at a time. If a recruiter thinks you are a better fit for a different team, they will reach out.

## Our interview process

Most roles follow the same four steps:

1. **Recruiter screen** (30 minutes, phone or video). We talk about the role, your background, and logistics like location and timing.
2. **Hiring manager conversation** (45 minutes, video).
3. **Technical or skills interview.** For engineers this is a practical exercise - for example debugging a misbehaving PLC program or reviewing a short pull request. For assembly roles it is a hands-on bench test at our Pittsburgh facility.
4. **On-site panel** in Pittsburgh: 3 to 4 conversations plus a tour of the assembly floor. We cover travel for candidates who live more than 100 miles away.

We aim to give every candidate a decision within 10 business days of the final interview.

## What we offer

- Medical, dental and vision coverage starting on your first day
- 401(k) with a company match
- Paid time off and paid parental leave (details are shared with your offer)
- A hybrid schedule for many office-based roles
- Relocation assistance for some roles - ask your recruiter
- Free safety boots and prescription safety glasses for anyone who works on the floor
- A training budget, and an internal Robotics 101 course for people in non-technical roles who want to learn how the arms work

## Early careers

We run a paid co-op program each spring and fall with local universities, mostly in mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering. Co-ops work on real projects with a named mentor. About half of our co-ops come back as full-time hires.
