=====================================================
1) TARGET
=====================================================
Ferrox Motion GmbH, Esslingen am Neckar (near Stuttgart), Germany.
Private, family-owned (second generation), ~140 employees.
Makes servo drives, compact planetary gearboxes and integrated joint actuators.
FY2025 revenue approx EUR 24.7M, EBITDA margin ~14%.

Ferrox has supplied the wrist gearboxes for ARC-6 and ARC-12 since 2019. They are a single-source supplier for those parts, and their 2025 price increase (+9%) is a big part of why ARC gross margin slipped last year.

=====================================================
2) PROPOSED TERMS (as of non-binding LOI, signed Sept 26)
=====================================================
- Headline price: $38.4 million enterprise value, cash-free / debt-free
- Consideration: 70% cash at close, 30% Acme stock
- Earn-out: up to an additional EUR 3M over 2 years tied to third-party actuator revenue (sellers asked for 5M; we held at 3M)
- Retention pool for key engineers: $1.5M over 3 years
- Founders' son (Matthias Ferrox, managing director) to stay on 3 years as head of Acme Europe drives business
- Exclusivity: 75 days from LOI

Financing: cash on hand plus a draw on the revolver; no new equity raise. Theresa's model shows net leverage staying under 1.5x after close.

=====================================================
3) WHY
=====================================================
 a. Vertical integration of the most expensive purchased component in the ARC arm. Finance estimates 260-310 bps of ARC gross margin improvement by year 2.
 b. Supply security - removes single-source risk on wrist gearboxes.
 c. In-house actuator know-how for the next-generation Kite cobot (integrated joints).
 d. A European foothold: engineering and service presence in Germany, which our sales team keeps asking for.
