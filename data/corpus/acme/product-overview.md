## The ARC family of industrial arms

The ARC family is our core product line: six-axis articulated arms built on a shared controller and a shared firmware platform (ArcOS).

| Model | Payload | Reach | Repeatability | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ARC-6 | 6 kg | 1,450 mm | +/- 0.03 mm | machine tending, pick and place |
| ARC-12 | 12 kg | 1,610 mm | +/- 0.04 mm | palletizing, light welding |
| ARC-20 | 20 kg | 1,820 mm | +/- 0.05 mm | heavy palletizing, press tending |

The ARC-6 has a rated payload of 6 kg and a reach of 1,450 mm, which makes it the most popular arm for CNC machine tending cells.

The ARC-20 is our heavy-payload arm. The ARC-20 has a rated payload of 20 kg and a reach of 1,820 mm.

All ARC arms are IP54 rated as standard, with an IP67 wrist option for washdown environments. They share the same teach pendant, the same tool flange (ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6), and the same spare parts kit for the controller, so a plant running a mix of models only has to stock one set of boards.

## Kite collaborative robot

Kite is our collaborative robot (cobot) for work alongside people without full guarding. Kite carries up to 5 kg, has built-in force and torque sensing in every joint, and is designed to be re-taught by hand-guiding: an operator grabs the wrist, moves it to a new point, and taps "save". Kite is popular for end-of-line case packing and for quality inspection stations.

## Tern autonomous mobile robot

Tern is an autonomous mobile robot (AMR) that moves material between work cells. The Tern AMR carries loads of up to 600 kg and navigates using onboard lidar, so no floor tape or reflectors are required. Tern docks automatically for charging and can run roughly 10 hours on a full charge in typical use. A Tern can be paired with an ARC-6 mounted on top for mobile machine tending.

## Software

**FleetSight** is our cloud fleet monitoring platform. Every connected Acme robot streams telemetry (joint temperatures, cycle counts, fault codes, uptime) to FleetSight, where plant managers can see dashboards, set alerts, and schedule preventive maintenance before a gearbox fails on a Friday night shift.

**CellForge** is our offline programming and simulation tool. Integrators can build a full work cell in CellForge, simulate cycle times, check for collisions, and push a program straight to the controller. CellForge runs on Windows workstations.

**ArcOS** is the firmware that runs on every ARC and Kite controller. ArcOS updates are delivered over the air through FleetSight for connected robots, or by USB for air-gapped sites.
