# FY2026 Salary Bands - US

Prepared by: Total Rewards (Mei-Ling Porter, Compensation Manager)
Reviewed by: Janelle Brooks, VP People; Theresa Kowalczyk, CFO
Last updated: 2026-02-27

## Summary

These are the base salary bands for US employees effective March 1, 2026, used for offers, promotions and the March merit cycle. Bands were refreshed against the Q4 2025 survey data for Pittsburgh-area and national manufacturing technology employers. On average bands moved up 3.8%; engineering bands moved more (about 5%) because we lost several controls engineers to competitors last year.

Do not share band information with candidates or employees outside of the approved talking points. Hiring managers can see the band for open roles they own in Workday; that's it.

## Rules for using the bands

1. New-hire offers should land between the band minimum and the midpoint. Offers above midpoint need VP approval; offers above the 90th percentile of the band need CFO approval.
2. Promotions move an employee to at least the minimum of the new band, with a typical increase of 8-12%.
3. Employees above band maximum are "red-circled": they keep their pay but receive merit increases as a lump sum rather than a base increase until the band catches up.
4. Mon Valley hourly roles use the same bands. We considered a lower geographic differential for McKeesport and decided against it for the launch year so we can staff up quickly.
5. Field roles receive a separate field premium of 6% of base, not reflected in the table.
