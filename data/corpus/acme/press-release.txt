ACME ROBOTICS TO OPEN MON VALLEY ASSEMBLY CENTER, ADDING 120 MANUFACTURING JOBS

New 140,000-square-foot facility in McKeesport will build the company's heavy-payload ARC-20 arm

PITTSBURGH, PA - June 9, 2026 - Acme Robotics, a Pittsburgh-based maker of industrial robots, today announced it will open the Mon Valley Assembly Center, a 140,000-square-foot assembly and test facility in McKeesport, Pennsylvania. The site is expected to begin production in October 2026.

The Mon Valley Assembly Center will add 120 new manufacturing jobs over its first two years, including assemblers, test technicians, quality inspectors, and maintenance staff. The facility will initially build the ARC-20 heavy-payload arm, which is currently assembled at the company's Strip District headquarters, freeing space there for Kite cobot and Tern AMR production.

"Our customers keep asking for shorter lead times on heavy arms, and we keep running out of room," said Margaret Okafor, Chief Executive Officer of Acme Robotics. "McKeesport gives us the floor space to roughly double ARC-20 output, and it lets us keep building in the region that taught this country how to make things."

The facility occupies a renovated former steel fabrication building near the Monongahela River. Renovation includes a new 40-ton overhead crane, two climate-controlled test bays where every arm runs a 48-hour burn-in before shipping, and a training classroom that will host customer operator courses.

Acme is partnering with a local community college to run a pre-employment robotics technician program. Graduates of the 10-week program will be guaranteed an interview for open roles at the Mon Valley site.

"Manufacturing jobs that pay well and need real skills are exactly what the Mon Valley needs," said a representative of the regional economic development council, which supported the project with a site-readiness grant.

Gus Ferraro, Acme's Director of Manufacturing, will lead the site. Hiring for the first 45 roles begins this summer through the Acme careers portal.

ABOUT ACME ROBOTICS
Acme Robotics designs and builds industrial robot arms, collaborative robots, and autonomous mobile robots for mid-size manufacturers, along with the FleetSight fleet monitoring platform and CellForge offline programming software. Founded in 2009 and headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, Acme employs about 850 people.
