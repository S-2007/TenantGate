Margaret, Theresa -

As discussed at the July 14 leadership offsite, this memo summarizes the current plan for the Q3 restructuring. The Q3 restructuring codename is PROJECT-BLUEHERON-ACME. Please use the codename in all calendar invites, email subjects and documents. Do not refer to "layoffs" or "RIF" in Slack, including DMs.

1. WHY
------
The legacy Wayfinder AGV line (wire-guided carts, sold 2014-2022) is being end-of-lifed now that Tern has replaced it in every new deal. Wayfinder service revenue fell 41% year over year in H1 and the support, refurbishment and spare-parts teams that serve it are significantly over capacity. At the same time, we are moving ARC-20 production to the Mon Valley Assembly Center, which leaves duplicated roles at HQ for that line.

We expect the combined actions to reduce annual operating expense by roughly $6.1M.

2. SCOPE
--------
The plan eliminates 64 roles in total, out of approximately 850 employees (about 7.5%).

  Group                                   Roles
  --------------------------------------  -----
  Wayfinder service & refurbishment         27
  Wayfinder spare parts / logistics         11
  HQ ARC-20 assembly (not transferring)     14
  Customer Support (legacy queue)            6
  Corporate G&A                              6
  --------------------------------------  -----
  TOTAL                                     64

Separately, we expect around 30 HQ ARC-20 assembly employees to accept transfers to Mon Valley. Those are not counted in the 64.

No reductions are planned in Robotics & Controls Engineering, FleetSight, or Field Integration.

3. TIMING
---------
  - Aug 4: final selection lists from department heads due to People Ops
  - Aug 11-15: legal / adverse impact review (outside counsel)
  - Aug 25: manager training (small group, under NDA)
  - Sept 15: notification day. All affected employees notified in person or by video between 9:00 and 12:00
  - Sept 15 - Nov 14: 60-day notice period. Because the HQ site action may reach WARN Act thresholds, all affected employees will receive 60 days of pay and benefits whether or not WARN technically applies.

4. SEVERANCE (proposed)
-----------------------
  - 2 weeks of base pay per year of service, minimum 6 weeks, maximum 26 weeks
  - COBRA premiums paid for 3 months
  - Pro-rated 2026 bonus for eligible employees, paid at the normal time
  - Outplacement services for 3 months
  - Severance conditioned on a signed separation agreement and release

Estimated one-time cost: $3.4M (severance, benefits continuation, outplacement, legal).

5. RISKS
--------
  a) Selection criteria for the HQ ARC-20 assembly group must be documented carefully; that group skews older and longer-tenured than the overall workforce. Outside counsel will run the adverse impact analysis.
  b) Customer risk: about 210 Wayfinder units are still in service at 19 customers. We will offer those customers a Tern trade-in program and a final 18-month parts supply through a third-party refurbisher.
  c) Leak risk ahead of Sept 15, especially given the Mon Valley press coverage. Keep the circle small.
