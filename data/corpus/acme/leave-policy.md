# Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-104 | **Effective:** January 1, 2026 | **Owner:** People Operations

## Purpose

This policy explains the paid and unpaid time off available to Acme Robotics employees in the US. It applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees working 20 or more hours a week receive prorated amounts.

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues each pay period, so you earn a little every two weeks rather than getting it all on January 1.

- New hires accrue PTO from their first day, prorated for the year.
- You may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Anything above that is forfeited on December 31 unless state law requires otherwise.
- PTO requests should be submitted in Workday at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than three days. Manufacturing employees should also coordinate with their production supervisor so shifts stay covered.
- During the annual inventory count week (usually the last week of June) and during scheduled customer commissioning windows, managers may restrict PTO for affected teams.

PTO is separate from company holidays. Acme observes 10 paid company holidays per year; the calendar is posted on the intranet each December.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 sick days per year, available in full on January 1 (prorated for new hires). Sick days can be used for your own illness or medical appointments, or to care for a sick family member.

Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out at separation. If you are out sick for more than three consecutive work days, People Operations may ask for a note from a healthcare provider.

Please let your manager or supervisor know as early as possible, and before the start of your shift if you work in manufacturing. Calling out sick is not a performance issue. Coming in sick to a shared assembly line is a problem.

## 3. Parental Leave

Acme provides 16 weeks paid parental leave to any employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption, or foster placement. This applies equally to all parents regardless of gender.

- Employees are eligible after 90 days of employment.
- Parental leave can be taken all at once or in up to two blocks, and must be used within 12 months of the birth or placement.
- Parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA where FMLA applies.
- Birthing parents may also be eligible for short-term disability before the parental leave period begins; People Operations will help coordinate.

Please give your manager and People Operations at least 30 days' notice when you can.

## 4. Other leave

| Type | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Bereavement | 5 days | immediate family; 2 days for extended family |
| Jury duty | as needed | paid at full salary for up to 15 days |
| Voting | up to 2 hours | if your shift doesn't allow time to vote |
| Military leave | per USERRA | plus 10 days of pay differential per year |
| Unpaid personal leave | up to 30 days | manager + People Ops approval |

## 5. Leave of absence and FMLA

Employees who have worked for Acme for at least 12 months and 1,250 hours may be eligible for up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act. Contact People Operations to start the paperwork; your health information is kept confidential and is not shared with your manager.

## 6. How to request leave

1. Submit the request in Workday under *Time Off*.
2. Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days.
3. For parental leave, FMLA, or anything longer than two weeks, People Operations will contact you to set up the paperwork.
