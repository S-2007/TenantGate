Q2 FY2026 FINANCIAL REPORT
==========================

Acme Robotics, Inc. | Quarter ended June 30, 2026
Prepared by: FP&A (Nikhil Rao, Director FP&A) | Approved: Theresa Kowalczyk, CFO
Issued: July 29, 2026

## 1. Headline numbers

Q2 revenue was $41.2 million, up 14% year over year and 6% above plan. Growth came mainly from ARC-12 palletizing cells and the first wave of the Halvorsen Packaging deployment.

| Metric | Q2 FY2026 | Q2 FY2025 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $41.2M | $36.1M | +14% |
| Gross margin | 38.6% | 40.1% | -150 bps |
| Operating expenses | $13.9M | $12.4M | +12% |
| Operating income | $2.0M | $2.1M | -5% |
| Adjusted EBITDA | $3.6M | $3.4M | +6% |
| Cash and equivalents (end of quarter) | $27.3M | $31.8M | |

## 2. Revenue by segment

| Segment | Q2 FY2026 | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Robot hardware (ARC, Kite, Tern) | $29.7M | 72% |
| Software (FleetSight, CellForge) | $5.1M | 12% |
| Services (integration, AcmeCare, parts) | $5.8M | 14% |
| Legacy Wayfinder service | $0.6M | 2% |

FleetSight annual recurring revenue reached $19.4M at quarter end, with 6,820 connected robots (up from 5,430 a year ago).

## 3. Customers

The top 10 customers accounted for 43% of Q2 revenue. Halvorsen Packaging was our largest customer in the quarter at 9.3% of revenue.

New logos in Q2: 23. Gross revenue retention for FleetSight: 96%.

## 4. Gross margin

Gross margin fell 150 basis points year over year to 38.6%. Drivers:

  - Wrist gearbox cost increase from our single-source supplier (approximately 90 bps)
  - Halvorsen discount pricing on the first wave of cells (approximately 40 bps)
  - Expedited freight on ARC-20 components in April (approximately 20 bps)

FleetSight gross margin held at 74%.

## 5. Operating expenses

- R&D: $6.2M (Kite next-gen joint program, FleetSight anomaly detection, ArcOS 4.8)
- Sales & marketing: $4.1M
- G&A: $3.6M, including about $0.3M of one-time costs from the April security incident response (outside forensics and legal)

Headcount at June 30: 853 (plan: 861).

## 6. Cash and capital

Cash decreased $3.1M in the quarter, driven by Mon Valley Assembly Center renovation capex ($4.4M in Q2) and higher inventory ahead of ARC-12 deliveries. The $20M revolving credit facility remains undrawn.

Inventory: $18.9M (up from $15.2M at March 31). Days sales outstanding increased to 52 days, mostly because of the Net 45 terms on the Halvorsen agreement.

## 7. Outlook

We are maintaining full-year revenue guidance of $162M-$168M. Q3 will include start-up costs for Mon Valley before production revenue begins in October. Management is evaluating cost actions related to the legacy Wayfinder business; details will be reviewed with the board separately.

## 8. Risks

1. Supplier concentration on wrist gearboxes.
2. Customer concentration - Halvorsen at 9.3% of revenue.
3. Mon Valley ramp schedule.
4. Steel and aluminum input costs.

_Figures are unaudited and subject to change during the year-end audit._
