# Leave and Time Off Policy

Policy number: HR-204 | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: People & Culture | Last reviewed 12-Dec-2025

## Purpose

This policy explains the paid and unpaid time off available to Northwind Health employees: paid time off (PTO), sick time, holidays, parental leave, bereavement leave, jury duty and other protected leaves. State law in Minnesota, Wisconsin or Iowa may give you additional rights; when it does, we follow the law.

## Who is covered

Full-time and part-time benefited employees. Per diem (PRN) and temporary employees receive sick time as required by state law but are not eligible for PTO or paid parental leave.

## PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO is for vacation, personal business, and anything else you want to use it for.

- PTO accrues each pay period (about 7.7 hours per biweekly paycheck for a full-time 40-hour employee). Part-time benefited employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.
- You may carry over up to 10 unused days into the next calendar year. Anything above that is forfeited on December 31 unless state law says otherwise.
- New employees may use PTO after 30 days of employment.
- Request PTO in Workday. Clinical staff should request planned PTO at least six weeks in advance; corporate staff at least two weeks.
- Accrued, unused PTO is paid out when you leave Northwind, as long as you give the required notice.

## SICK TIME

Separate from PTO, full-time employees also receive 12 sick days per year (part-time employees receive a prorated amount). Sick time may be used for:

1. your own illness, injury, or medical appointment;
2. caring for a sick family member;
3. safety leave related to domestic abuse, sexual assault or stalking.

Sick time does not pay out when you leave. Unused sick time carries over up to a maximum balance of 20 days. If you are out sick for more than three consecutive scheduled shifts, your manager may ask for a note from a clinician. Clinical staff with fever or vomiting must not work in patient areas and should call the clinic manager before the shift starts.

## Holidays

Northwind observes eight paid holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Clinical staff who work on a holiday receive holiday pay at 1.5x plus a floating holiday to use later.

## PARENTAL LEAVE

Eligible employees receive 18 weeks paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Details:

- Eligibility: 6 months of continuous employment by the date leave begins.
- Pay is 100% of base pay for the full 18 weeks.
- Both parents are eligible if both work at Northwind.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement and may be taken in up to two blocks.
- Parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA and applicable state leave.
- Birthing parents may also receive short-term disability benefits; these coordinate with (not stack on top of) parental leave pay.

## Bereavement

Up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member; up to 2 paid days for other relatives.

## Jury duty and voting

Northwind pays your regular base pay for up to 10 days of jury service per year. Employees may take up to two paid hours to vote in state and federal elections if their shift does not allow time to vote.

## Unpaid and protected leaves

- **FMLA**: up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave for eligible employees.
- **Military leave**: as required by USERRA.
- **Personal leave of absence**: up to 30 days unpaid with approval from your manager and HR.

## How to request leave

1. Submit the request in Workday under Time Off.
2. For any leave longer than 5 days (parental, FMLA, medical), also open a case with our leave administrator through the People Help Center.
3. Talk to your manager early, especially for clinical roles that need coverage.

Questions: People Help Center in ServiceNow.
