# Northwind Health - Who We Are and What We Do

_Last updated: March 2026_

Northwind Health is an outpatient care organization headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Northwind Health operates 34 outpatient clinics across Minnesota, Wisconsin, and Iowa, and delivers virtual care through our telehealth service, Northwind Anywhere. About 2,300 people work at Northwind, including roughly 780 physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, and nurses.

We were founded in 2009 as a group of four family-medicine practices in the Twin Cities. The idea was simple then and it is still simple now: make it easy to see a clinician you trust, close to home or from home, without waiting three weeks for an appointment.

## What we offer

Our clinics are grouped into "care hubs." A hub is a larger clinic with lab and imaging on site, surrounded by smaller neighborhood clinics that refer into it.

| Service line | Available at | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary care (adult and family medicine) | All 34 clinics | Same-week appointments at most sites |
| Urgent care | 11 clinics | Open until 9:00 p.m. on weekdays, 6:00 p.m. on weekends |
| Pediatrics | 19 clinics + telehealth | Well-child visits, vaccines, sick visits |
| Women's health | 14 clinics | OB/GYN, prenatal care, contraception counseling |
| Behavioral health | 9 clinics + telehealth | Therapy, psychiatry, medication management |
| Lab and imaging | 8 care hubs | X-ray, ultrasound, walk-in lab draws |
| Chronic care programs | All clinics + telehealth | Diabetes, hypertension, COPD |

Specialty referrals (cardiology, orthopedics, oncology and so on) go to partner health systems; we do not run hospitals or inpatient units.

## Northwind Anywhere (telehealth)

Northwind Anywhere is our virtual care service. Patients use it from the Northwind Anywhere app (iOS and Android) or from a web browser. It includes:

* **Video visits** with a Northwind clinician, usually the same day.
* **E-visits** - a short questionnaire for simple problems such as a urinary tract infection, pink eye or a medication refill, answered by a clinician within four business hours.
* **Remote patient monitoring (RPM)** - connected blood pressure cuffs and glucose meters that send readings to a nurse care team. Roughly 6,200 patients were enrolled in RPM at the end of 2025.
* **Secure messaging** with your care team, plus lab results, after-visit summaries and appointment booking.

Video visits are available to patients physically located in Minnesota, Wisconsin, or Iowa at the time of the visit, because our clinicians are licensed in those three states.

## Who we serve

In 2025 Northwind clinics and Northwind Anywhere together handled about 1.1 million patient visits, and 27% of those visits were virtual. Our patients include families with commercial insurance, Medicare and Medicaid members, and people paying out of pocket. We also run on-site clinics for several large employers in the Minneapolis-St. Paul area.

## How we measure quality

We publish a short quality scorecard every year. A few of the measures we track closely:

1. Time to the third next available primary care appointment (goal: under 5 days)
2. Controlled blood pressure rate for patients with hypertension
3. Childhood immunization status at age 2
4. Patient experience scores (we survey every patient after every visit)
5. Percentage of urgent care visits completed in under 60 minutes

## Technology, briefly

Every Northwind clinic shares one electronic health record, which means your chart follows you whether you walk into our St. Cloud clinic or open a video visit from your kitchen in Dubuque. Northwind Anywhere is built and operated by our own Digital Health team in Minneapolis, with video provided by a HIPAA-compliant partner.
