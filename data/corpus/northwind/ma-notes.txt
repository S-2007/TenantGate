Working notes - acquisition evaluation
Codename: PROJECT-OSPREY-NORTHWIND
Deal team: Daniel Okafor (CFO, sponsor), Lena Hartmann (VP Corporate Development), Rachel Steen (General Counsel), Dr. Samuel Achebe (CMO, clinical diligence)
Updated 2026-08-27 (LH)

----------------------------------------------------------------
TARGET
----------------------------------------------------------------

Target: Cedar Valley Clinics, a physician-owned primary care and urgent care group in eastern Iowa and southwest Wisconsin.

  - 9 clinics (Cedar Rapids x3, Iowa City x2, Dubuque, Davenport, Platteville WI, Prairie du Chien WI)
  - ~410 employees, 58 physicians and APPs
  - 2025 revenue approx. $72.5M, adjusted EBITDA approx. $6.9M
  - Payer mix: 52% commercial, 29% Medicare/MA, 17% Medicaid, 2% self-pay
  - Using an older EHR that is end-of-support in 2028

Why it fits:
  1. Fills our gap in eastern Iowa, where we have only our Dubuque clinic today; their Dubuque clinic would be consolidated into ours.
  2. Their physicians want telehealth; they have none. Northwind Anywhere could launch to their ~96,000 active patients in about 6 months after close.
  3. Strong urgent care volumes (their Cedar Rapids urgent cares see ~180 patients/day combined).
  4. Opportunity to move them onto our EHR and cut their IT spend by roughly half.

----------------------------------------------------------------
VALUATION / OFFER
----------------------------------------------------------------

Indicative offer submitted Aug 19: total enterprise value of $61 million, cash, subject to diligence. That is about 8.8x 2025 adjusted EBITDA, in line with recent Midwest primary care deals (range 7.5x - 10x).

Structure:
  - 85% cash at close
  - 15% earnout over 2 years tied to physician retention (at least 90% of physicians still employed at 24 months) and urgent care volume
  - Physician owners roll into Northwind employment agreements with 3-year terms and non-competes where enforceable (note: enforceability varies by state - GC reviewing)

Financing: cash on hand plus a draw on the revolving credit facility. No equity issuance needed.

Synergies (deal team estimate, year 3):
  - EHR/IT consolidation: $2.1M
  - Revenue cycle onto our platform: $1.4M
  - Supply chain / purchasing: $0.6M
  - Telehealth revenue uplift: $1.8M
  TOTAL: ~$5.9M run-rate

RISKS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  * Physician departures after close (biggest risk; earnout is meant to address it).
  * EHR migration fatigue - their staff went through a painful upgrade in 2023.
  * Competitive tension: at least one other regional health system has approached them. Their banker hinted a second bid is "in the mid-60s."
  * Integration bandwidth: overlaps with Q3 workforce restructuring work. Integration team must be ring-fenced.
