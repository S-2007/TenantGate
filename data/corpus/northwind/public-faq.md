# Frequently Asked Questions

*Updated Aug 2026. Can't find your answer? Call the Patient Support Line at 1-800-555-0147.*

---

### APPOINTMENTS

**How do I book an appointment?**
You can book through the Northwind Anywhere app, online at northwindhealth.example, or by calling your clinic directly. Most primary care clinics offer same-week appointments.

**Do you take walk-ins?**
Our 11 urgent care clinics accept walk-ins. Primary care clinics are appointment-only, but we keep some same-day slots open each morning - call at 8:00 a.m. for the best chance.

**What is your cancellation policy?**
Please cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. If you miss an appointment or cancel with less than 24 hours notice three times in 12 months, we may ask you to book with a care coordinator before scheduling again. We do not charge a no-show fee.

**What should I bring to my first visit?**
A photo ID, your insurance card, a list of your current medications, and any records from your previous clinician if you have them handy.

---

### TELEHEALTH (NORTHWIND ANYWHERE)

**When can I do a video visit?**
Northwind Anywhere video visits are available from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Central Time, seven days a week. E-visits can be submitted any time and are answered within four business hours.

**Do I need to be in a certain place for a video visit?**
Yes. You must be physically located in Minnesota, Wisconsin, or Iowa during the visit. This is a state licensing rule, not ours.

**What if my connection drops during a visit?**
Your clinician will try to reconnect for up to five minutes, then call you on the phone number in your profile to finish the visit.

**Can I see my regular doctor by video?**
Often, yes. Many Northwind primary care clinicians hold scheduled video appointments. On-demand video visits are with whichever clinician is available.

### BILLING & INSURANCE

**Which insurance plans do you accept?**
We accept most major commercial health plans, Medicare, Medicare Advantage plans from several carriers, and Medicaid in Minnesota, Wisconsin and Iowa. Plans change every year, so please check the "Insurance we accept" page or call us before your visit.

**I don't have insurance. Can I still be seen?**
Absolutely. Self-pay prices are posted on our website. A standard video visit is $49 and an urgent care visit starts at $135. Ask about our financial assistance program if cost is a concern.

**Why did I get a bill after my visit?**
Your plan may apply a copay, coinsurance, or deductible. Your bill will show what your insurer paid. Call Patient Financial Services at 1-800-555-0162 with questions.

---

### PRIVACY & RECORDS

**How do I get a copy of my medical records?**
Most records are available instantly in the Northwind Anywhere app. For a full copy, submit a records request form; we fulfill requests within 15 business days.

**Who can see my information?**
Only the people involved in your care, billing, and the operations of our clinics, as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices. We never sell patient data.

**Can my spouse or adult child access my chart?**
Yes, if you grant them proxy access in the app. You can revoke proxy access any time.

---

### OTHER

**Do you have a pharmacy?**
Four of our care hubs have an on-site retail pharmacy. Prescriptions can be sent to any pharmacy you choose.

**Are your clinics accessible?**
All Northwind clinics are wheelchair accessible. Interpreter services (including ASL) are free; let us know when you book.
