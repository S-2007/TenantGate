# NORTHWIND HEALTH EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Version 7.2 | Owner: People & Culture (Teresa Vang, Chief People Officer) | last updated 02/03/2026

> This handbook applies to every Northwind Health employee - clinical and non-clinical, full-time and part-time - in all three states where we operate. Where state law gives you more protection than this handbook, state law wins. Specific policies (leave, expenses, remote work, IT security) live in their own documents and are linked from the intranet; this handbook summarizes them and points you there.

---

## 1. Welcome

Northwind Health exists to make good outpatient care easy to reach. We have about 2,300 employees working in 34 clinics, the Minneapolis contact center, the Digital Health office in the North Loop, and from home. Whatever your role is, you are part of a patient's experience, even if you never meet that patient.

Our values, which you will hear about constantly:

1. **Patients first, always.** When in doubt, do what is best for the patient in front of you.
2. **Say it plainly.** No jargon with patients, no politics with colleagues.
3. **Own the follow-up.** If you touched it, close the loop.
4. **Get a little better every week.**

## 2. Employment basics

### 2.1 Employment categories
- *Full-time*: scheduled 36 or more hours per week (many nursing schedules are 3 x 12-hour shifts).
- *Part-time benefited*: 20-35 hours per week.
- *Per diem / PRN*: scheduled as needed, not eligible for most benefits.
- *Temporary*: a defined end date, usually under six months.

### 2.2 Introductory period
All new employees have a 90-day introductory period. Your manager will meet with you at 30, 60 and 90 days. Clinical staff also complete competency validation with a clinical educator during this time.

### 2.3 Working hours and scheduling
Clinic hours vary by site. Clinical schedules are posted in the scheduling system (ShiftBoard) at least four weeks in advance. Shift swaps must be approved by your clinic manager in ShiftBoard - a text message to a coworker is not a swap.

For corporate and Digital Health staff: Core collaboration hours are 9:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Central Time, Monday through Friday. Outside those hours, schedule flexibly with your manager.

### 2.4 Timekeeping
Non-exempt employees must clock in and out for every shift, including meal breaks. Working "off the clock" is not allowed, ever, even if you volunteer to. If you forget to clock in, fix it in ShiftBoard within 48 hours and your manager will approve the correction.

## 3. Pay

Pay is biweekly, every other Friday, by direct deposit. Pay ranges for every role are reviewed annually by People & Culture. Merit increases take effect the first full pay period of April. Shift differentials apply to clinical evening, weekend and holiday shifts; current rates are on the intranet under "Pay & Differentials."

Overtime for non-exempt employees is paid at 1.5x for hours over 40 in a workweek. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager, except in a patient-safety situation.

## 4. Benefits (summary)

Details are in the Benefits Guide you receive at hire and during open enrollment.

- Medical, dental and vision insurance begin on your first day of employment.
- Life and disability insurance are provided at no cost to full-time employees.
- Paid time off, sick time and parental leave are covered in the Leave Policy.
- Free Northwind Anywhere visits for you and your dependents.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) through our partner, available 24/7.
- Continuing education allowance for licensed clinicians (amount varies by license type).

Open enrollment runs every year from November 1 through November 15.

## 5. Conduct

### 5.1 Professional conduct
We expect courtesy toward patients, families and coworkers. Harassment, discrimination, threats and retaliation are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, any HR Business Partner, or anonymously to the Integrity Line at 1-800-555-0199.

### 5.2 Dress code
Clinical staff wear Northwind-issued scrubs in the color assigned to their role (navy for RNs, ceil blue for MAs, charcoal for providers). ID badges must be visible above the waist at all times in clinical areas. Corporate staff: business casual on days you are in the office.

### 5.3 Patient privacy
Every employee must follow HIPAA and our Patient Privacy Procedures. Accessing a patient chart without a work reason - including your own family members, coworkers, or a local celebrity - is a serious violation and may lead to termination. Our EHR logs every chart access and the Privacy Office audits those logs.

### 5.4 Social media
Never post anything about patients, even without names. Do not take photos in clinical areas. You may say you work at Northwind, but do not speak for Northwind unless Communications has asked you to.

### 5.5 Gifts
Employees may not accept cash or gift cards from patients or vendors. Small perishable gifts (cookies, flowers) given to a whole team are fine.

### 5.6 Conflicts of interest
Disclose outside jobs, board seats, or financial interests in vendors in the annual conflict of interest attestation.

## 6. Health and safety

- Report any workplace injury or needle-stick to your manager and Employee Health within 24 hours.
- Annual influenza vaccination is required for all employees who work in a clinic, unless you have an approved medical or religious exemption.
- TB screening is required at hire for clinical staff.
- Workplace violence: if you feel unsafe, call 911 first, then notify your clinic manager. Every clinic has a panic button at the front desk.

## 7. Leave and time off (summary)

See the **Leave Policy** for the full rules, including how PTO accrues, sick time, parental leave and holidays. Requests go through Workday. Clinical staff should submit PTO requests at least six weeks in advance for planned time off so schedules can be covered.

## 8. Travel and expenses (summary)

See the **Expense Policy**. Short version: book travel through Navan, keep your receipts, and submit expense reports in Workday within 30 days.

## 9. Technology and security (summary)

See the **IT Security Policy**. You are responsible for every action taken under your login. Never share passwords, never approve an MFA prompt you did not start, and report suspicious email using the "Report Phish" button in Outlook.

## 10. Working remotely (summary)

Some roles are eligible for hybrid work; see the **Remote Work Policy**. Clinical roles that require hands-on patient care are not eligible for remote work, though some clinicians have scheduled telehealth shifts from home.

## 11. Performance and development

Northwind runs one formal performance review cycle each year, between January and February, with a mid-year check-in in July. Reviews use a written self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback for most roles. Your goals live in Workday.

Internal transfers: you may apply for an internal job after 6 months in your current role, as long as you are not on a performance improvement plan. Tell your manager before you interview.

## 12. Leaving Northwind

We ask for two weeks notice for most roles and four weeks for licensed clinicians and people managers. Return all equipment, badges and scrubs on or before your last day. Unused accrued PTO is paid out according to the Leave Policy and state law.

## 13. Required training

Annual compliance training must be completed by November 30 each year. It covers HIPAA privacy and security, fraud waste and abuse, harassment prevention, and workplace safety. Clinical staff also complete annual BLS recertification as their license requires. Employees who miss the deadline lose access to the EHR until training is complete.

## 14. Questions

Ask your manager, your HR Business Partner, or open a ticket in the People Help Center in ServiceNow. We update this handbook at least once a year; the version on the intranet is the official one.
