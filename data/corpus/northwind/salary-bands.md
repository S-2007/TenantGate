# FY2026 Salary Bands (Base Pay)

Prepared by: Total Rewards (Owen Brekke, Director of Compensation)
Reviewed by: Teresa Vang, Chief People Officer
Effective: April 6, 2026 (merit cycle) | last updated 3/18/26

## How to read this

- Bands are **base pay only**. Shift differentials, on-call pay, sign-on bonuses and the clinician productivity incentive are excluded.
- Ranges are for the Minneapolis-St. Paul metro. Apply the geographic factor for other locations: **Rural WI/IA = 0.93**, **Rochester MN / Madison WI = 0.98**.
- Hourly roles are shown as annualized base at 2,080 hours for comparison; actual pay is hourly.
- Midpoint = market 50th percentile from the 2025 healthcare comp survey, aged 3.5%.
- New hires should normally be offered between the minimum and the midpoint. Offers above midpoint need HRBP + Total Rewards approval; offers above the 90th percentile of the band need CPO approval.

## Changes from FY2025

1. RN I-III bands moved up 5.5% (market pressure; RN vacancy rate hit 14% in Q4 2025).
2. Medical Assistant I minimum raised to $19.50/hour ($40,560 annualized) to stay ahead of local minimum wage increases.
3. New band added for Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner to support behavioral health expansion.
4. Software engineering bands moved up only 2%. Market is flat.

## Pay equity review

The 2025 pay equity analysis (outside counsel, privileged) found no statistically significant unexplained gap by gender or race in any job family with more than 30 incumbents. Six individual adjustments were made, totaling $41,300, and are included in the April merit budget.

## Merit budget

The FY2026 merit pool is 3.5% of base payroll for eligible employees, plus a 0.5% pool for market adjustments managed centrally by Total Rewards. Managers receive their allocations in Workday on March 2.
