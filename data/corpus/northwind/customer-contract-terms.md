Payer & Employer Contract Terms Summary
=======================================

Maintained by: Managed Care Contracting (Grace Holmquist, Director) with Finance

This summary covers our largest value-based and direct contracts where Northwind provides services to a health plan or employer customer. Fee schedules themselves are in the contracting system (Contract Manager); this page is the cheat sheet for Finance forecasting and for Legal renewals.

## 1. Lakeshore County Health Plan (LCHP) - Telehealth & Primary Care Services Agreement

**Customer:** LCHP, a county-sponsored Medicaid managed care and employee health plan with about 148,000 members.
**Term:** 3 years, January 1, 2026 - December 31, 2028. Auto-renews for 1-year terms unless either party gives 120 days notice.
**Services:** Northwind Anywhere video visits and e-visits for all LCHP members, plus in-person primary care at 12 designated Northwind clinics.

Commercial terms:

- LCHP receives a 12% discount off Northwind's standard telehealth fee schedule for video visits and e-visits.
- In-person primary care is paid at 104% of the state Medicaid fee schedule.
- Per-member-per-month (PMPM) care coordination fee: $3.15 PMPM for members attributed to a Northwind primary care clinician.
- Quality bonus pool: up to $1.2 million per contract year if HEDIS targets are met (controlling high blood pressure, diabetes HbA1c control, well-child visits).

Service level terms:

- Northwind commits to a 99.9% uptime SLA for the Northwind Anywhere platform, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance (max 4 hours per month).
- SLA credits: if monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, LCHP receives a credit of 5% of that month's telehealth fees; below 99.5%, 10%; below 99.0%, 20%. Credits are capped at 20% of monthly telehealth fees and are LCHP's sole remedy for downtime.
- Average speed to connect for on-demand video visits: under 20 minutes for 90% of visits, reported quarterly. No credit attached; three missed quarters in a row is a material breach.

Data and privacy: Business Associate Agreement executed 2025-11-20. Monthly claims and encounter files via SFTP.

Contract value forecast (FY2026): approximately $19.4 million, net of discount.

Note from Finance (D. Okafor): the March 2026 security incident did not cause downtime, so no SLA credit applied, but LCHP asked for a written summary. Legal sent it April 20.

## 2. Twin Lakes Manufacturing - Onsite Employer Clinic

- Customer: Twin Lakes Manufacturing (about 3,100 employees, Shakopee MN)
- Term: Jul 1 2024 - Jun 30 2027
- Fee: fixed management fee of $86,000/month plus pass-through of supplies and lab costs
- Staffing: 1 NP, 2 MAs, 0.5 FTE physician oversight; clinic open 40 hours/week
- Performance guarantee: 15% of the annual management fee at risk if employee satisfaction drops below 85% or same-day access below 90%
- Renewal talks start January 2027

## 3. Prairie Benefit Trust - Behavioral Health Access

* Customer: Prairie Benefit Trust (multi-employer union health fund, WI)
* Telehealth behavioral health visits at contracted case rate of $142 per therapy visit, $210 per psychiatry visit
* Access guarantee: new patient appointment within 10 business days; credit of $25 per member for each missed guarantee, billed quarterly
* Term through Dec 31 2027
