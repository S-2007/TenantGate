New Hire Onboarding Guide
=========================

Welcome to Northwind Health! This guide walks you through your first two weeks. Keep it handy - your onboarding buddy will refer to it.

last updated: Jan 12 2026 - People & Culture / Learning team

## Before day one

You should have received:

- An offer letter and a link to complete pre-hire paperwork in Workday (I-9 documents, tax forms, direct deposit).
- For clinical staff: a request to upload your license, certifications and immunization records for credentialing.
- A welcome email from your manager with your start time and location.

If anything is missing, email onboarding@northwindhealth.example.

## DAY ONE

Most new hires start on a Monday with **New Employee Orientation (NEO)**, held at the Minneapolis Support Center (2nd floor, Lake Room) from 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Remote corporate hires join NEO by video.

What happens on day one:

1. Badge photo and ID badge pickup (bring a photo ID).
2. Laptop pickup for corporate staff; clinical staff receive workstation login instead.
3. IT account activation. You must enroll in Okta Verify multi-factor authentication before the end of your first day. Without MFA, you will be locked out of email, Workday and the EHR the next morning.
4. HIPAA basics and the Northwind values presentation.
5. Lunch with your cohort (on us).
6. Benefits overview. Remember that you have 30 days from your start date to make benefit elections in Workday.

## Week one

| Day | Corporate / Digital Health | Clinical |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | NEO | NEO |
| Tue | Meet your team, set up laptop tools | EHR training (full day, classroom) |
| Wed | 1:1 with manager, review 30-60-90 plan | EHR training continued, clinic tour |
| Thu | Shadow a clinic for a half day | Shadow shift with preceptor |
| Fri | Required online modules | Competency check-offs with clinical educator |

Every new hire spends at least a half day in a clinic during the first two weeks. Yes, even engineers and accountants. It is the fastest way to understand what we do.

## Week two

- Finish the required online modules in Workday Learning (HIPAA privacy, information security awareness, harassment prevention, workplace safety). These are due by the end of your 10th working day.
- Clinical staff begin working with a preceptor. Typical orientation length: 4 weeks for MAs, 6 weeks for RNs, 8 weeks for new graduate RNs.
- Corporate staff finalize their first-quarter goals with their manager.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from their department - someone who has been here at least a year. Your buddy is not your manager. They are the person you ask "where is the good coffee" and "what does RCM stand for" (revenue cycle management).

## Systems you will use

* **Workday** - HR, pay, time off, learning, expenses.
* **ShiftBoard** - schedules and shift swaps (clinical).
* **Outlook and Teams** - email and meetings. Slack is used by Digital Health and IT only.
* **ServiceNow** - IT and People Help Center tickets.
* **The EHR** - access is role-based; your manager requests it.
* **Okta** - single sign-on for almost everything.

## Getting help

- IT Service Desk: ext. 4357 (HELP) or ServiceNow, 24/7 for clinical issues.
- People Help Center: ServiceNow, business hours.
- Payroll questions: payroll@northwindhealth.example.

## 30-60-90

At 30 days: you know your team, your systems, and who to ask.
At 60 days: you can handle most of your normal work independently.
At 90 days: you finish your introductory period and have a formal check-in with your manager.

Welcome aboard - we are glad you are here.
