# Q2 FY2026 Financial Report

Prepared by: Finance (Daniel Okafor, CFO; Mei Lin Ostrowski, Controller)
Period: April 1 - June 30, 2026
Date issued: July 21, 2026

---

## Highlights

- Q2 revenue was $187.6 million, up 9.4% from Q2 FY2025 and 1.8% above budget.
- Operating income of $9.1 million (4.9% operating margin), versus budget of $8.3 million.
- Total visits: 291,400 (in-person 206,900; virtual 84,500). Virtual share rose to 29% from 26% a year ago.
- Cash and investments at quarter end: $142.7 million (about 71 days cash on hand).
- Commercial reimbursement in Minnesota continues to lag plan.

## Revenue by service line

| Service line | Q2 FY26 ($M) | Q2 FY25 ($M) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary care | 84.9 | 79.3 | +7.1% |
| Urgent care | 26.2 | 24.8 | +5.6% |
| Telehealth (Northwind Anywhere) | 31.7 | 25.0 | +26.8% |
| Women's health | 14.6 | 13.9 | +5.0% |
| Behavioral health | 12.3 | 10.1 | +21.8% |
| Lab & imaging | 11.4 | 11.0 | +3.6% |
| Employer & other contracts | 6.5 | 7.4 | -12.2% |
| **Total** | **187.6** | **171.5** | **+9.4%** |

Telehealth growth was driven by the new payer contracts signed in Q1 and by a 34% increase in e-visits. The employer contract decline reflects the loss of one onsite clinic contract that ended March 31.

## Expenses

| Category | Q2 FY26 ($M) | % of revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Salaries, wages & benefits | 121.4 | 64.7% |
| Contract labor (agency nurses) | 4.8 | 2.6% |
| Supplies & pharmaceuticals | 15.9 | 8.5% |
| Occupancy | 11.2 | 6.0% |
| Technology & software | 13.6 | 7.2% |
| Purchased services & other | 11.6 | 6.2% |
| **Total operating expenses** | **178.5** | **95.1%** |

Agency nurse spending dropped from $7.9M in Q2 FY25 to $4.8M thanks to the RN band increases and the new graduate residency program. Technology spending includes $1.1M of one-time costs from the March security incident response (forensics, notifications, outside counsel).

## Payer mix (gross revenue)

- Commercial: 47%
- Medicare and Medicare Advantage: 26%
- Medicaid: 21%
- Self-pay and other: 6%

## Balance sheet and cash

Days in accounts receivable improved to 38.2 from 41.5 at year end, mostly because the new claim scrubbing rules in the clearinghouse cut first-pass denials from 9.8% to 7.1%. Capital spending in the quarter was $6.3M, mainly the Eau Claire clinic build-out and laptop refresh.

The revolving credit facility ($75M) remains undrawn.

## Full-year outlook

We are holding the FY2026 revenue forecast at $748 million. Operating margin is forecast at 4.2%, below the 5.0% budget, because of the Minnesota commercial rate shortfall and continued wage pressure. Management has started a cost program in Q3 to close the gap; details are being shared with the board separately.

## Key risks

1. Minnesota commercial rates (about 4% below plan).
2. Medicaid redetermination reducing covered patients in Wisconsin.
3. Labor costs, especially RN and behavioral health clinician wages.
4. Cyber risk and regulatory exposure following the March incident.

Prepared for the July 28, 2026 board meeting.
