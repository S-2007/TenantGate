NORTHWIND HEALTH
BOARD OF DIRECTORS - MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
CONFIDENTIAL

Date:      July 28, 2026
Time:      1:00 p.m. - 4:40 p.m. CT
Location:  Minneapolis Support Center, Board Room (4th floor), with video option

Directors present: Catherine Wahl (Chair), Margaret Lindqvist (CEO), Dr. Harold Benn, Sofia Ruiz-Taylor, Patrick O'Connell, Dr. Linnea Ekholm, James Whitebird
Directors absent: none
Also present: Daniel Okafor (CFO), Rachel Steen (General Counsel, acting as Secretary), Elena Sorensen (CTO), Jordan Pike (CISO, item 4 only), Teresa Vang (CPO, executive session only)

-------------------------------------------------------------
1. CALL TO ORDER AND APPROVAL OF MINUTES
-------------------------------------------------------------

The Chair called the meeting to order at 1:02 p.m. and confirmed a quorum. The minutes of the April 21, 2026 meeting were approved as distributed (motion: Ruiz-Taylor; second: O'Connell; unanimous).

-------------------------------------------------------------
2. CEO REPORT
-------------------------------------------------------------

Ms. Lindqvist reported on operations for the quarter. Highlights:

  - Patient experience scores held at 4.6/5.
  - Third-next-available primary care appointment improved to 4.1 days (goal under 5).
  - The pediatric telehealth service is on track for an early October launch, following the Rochester pilot.
  - RN vacancy rate fell to 9.8% from 14% at year end, after the band increases approved in March.
  - The Eau Claire, WI clinic remains on schedule to open in January 2027.

Dr. Benn asked about physician burnout survey results. Ms. Lindqvist said results will be presented at the October meeting with an action plan from the CMO.

-------------------------------------------------------------
3. FINANCE REPORT
-------------------------------------------------------------

Mr. Okafor presented the Q2 financial report (distributed in advance). Revenue and operating income were above budget for the quarter, but the full-year operating margin forecast was reduced to 4.2% due to commercial rate shortfalls in Minnesota.

Mr. O'Connell (Audit & Finance Committee chair) noted the committee had reviewed the results on July 22 and recommended acceptance.

RESOLVED: The board accepts the Q2 FY2026 financial report. (Unanimous.)

Capital request: Mr. Okafor requested approval of $4.6 million for the Eau Claire clinic equipment and IT fit-out, previously included in the capital plan as a placeholder of $4.0 million. The increase reflects imaging equipment pricing.

RESOLVED: The board approves capital spending of up to $4.6 million for the Eau Claire clinic. (Motion: Ekholm; second: Whitebird; 6 in favor, 1 abstention - Ruiz-Taylor, citing a family member's employment with the equipment vendor.)

-------------------------------------------------------------
4. CYBERSECURITY AND MARCH INCIDENT FOLLOW-UP
-------------------------------------------------------------

Mr. Pike reviewed the status of action items from the March scheduling API incident. Five of seven items are complete; the default-deny authorization change is in progress and due July 31, and the third-party penetration test is scheduled for September. Total incident cost to date is approximately $1.3 million including forensics, notification, counsel and engineering time. No regulatory penalties have been assessed; the HHS Office for Civil Rights inquiry remains open.

Ms. Wahl asked whether cyber insurance would cover the costs. Mr. Pike reported that the insurer has accepted the claim subject to the $500,000 retention. The policy renews on October 1; the broker expects a premium increase of 15-20%.

The board asked management to present a two-year security roadmap and budget at the October meeting.

-------------------------------------------------------------
5. TECHNOLOGY UPDATE
-------------------------------------------------------------

Ms. Sorensen presented the Northwind Anywhere roadmap: pediatric launch, a Spanish-language app experience (Q1 2027), and expanding remote patient monitoring to heart failure patients. She also noted the legacy patient portal will be retired by December 2026 now that all patients have been migrated to the app.

-------------------------------------------------------------
6. EXECUTIVE SESSION
-------------------------------------------------------------

At 3:35 p.m. the board went into executive session with the CEO, CFO, CPO and General Counsel to discuss (a) a confidential workforce plan and (b) a potential strategic transaction. Discussion and materials for these items are recorded in separate privileged minutes maintained by the General Counsel.

Upon returning to open session at 4:30 p.m., the Chair reported that the board had authorized management to proceed with the workforce plan as presented, and had authorized continued due diligence on the strategic transaction, with a decision expected at a special session in October.

-------------------------------------------------------------
7. OTHER BUSINESS
-------------------------------------------------------------

  - The Governance Committee will propose a new director with health plan experience at the October meeting.
  - Next regular meeting: October 27, 2026.

-------------------------------------------------------------
8. ADJOURNMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:40 p.m.
