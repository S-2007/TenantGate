Northwind Health Launches Same-Day Pediatric Telehealth Across Minnesota, Wisconsin and Iowa

New "Northwind Anywhere Kids" service connects families with a pediatric clinician by video, seven days a week

MINNEAPOLIS, October 6, 2026 -- Northwind Health, an outpatient care and telehealth organization with 34 clinics in the Upper Midwest, today announced the launch of Northwind Anywhere Kids, a same-day pediatric video visit service available to families anywhere in Minnesota, Wisconsin and Iowa.

Parents can open the Northwind Anywhere app, choose "My child is sick," and typically be connected to a pediatric nurse practitioner or pediatrician in under 30 minutes. The service handles common childhood illnesses such as ear pain, fevers, rashes, coughs and minor injuries, and it can send prescriptions to a local pharmacy and schedule an in-person follow-up at one of Northwind's 19 pediatric clinics when needed.

"Every parent knows the feeling of a sick kid at 6 p.m. and a pediatrician's office that just closed," said Dr. Samuel Achebe, Chief Medical Officer of Northwind Health. "Northwind Anywhere Kids is built so that families can get a trusted answer quickly, without driving to an emergency room for something that can be handled from the couch."

Key facts about the launch:

  * Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Central Time, every day including holidays.
  * Ages: newborns over 2 weeks old through age 17.
  * Cost: covered by most commercial plans and by Medicaid in all three states; the self-pay price is $49 per visit.
  * Staffing: a dedicated team of 42 pediatric clinicians at launch.

Northwind Health plans to hire 60 additional pediatric clinicians by mid-2027 to support the service and its in-person pediatric clinics, with a focus on rural communities in western Wisconsin and northern Iowa where families often drive more than an hour to see a pediatrician.

During a pilot in the Rochester, Minnesota area this spring, 81% of pediatric video visits were resolved without the child needing an in-person visit within the following 72 hours, and parents rated the experience 4.8 out of 5 on average.

"We designed the visit flow with parents in the room," said Helen Brandt, Vice President of Clinical Operations. "A lot of the work was making the app simple enough to use with one hand while holding a toddler."

Northwind Anywhere Kids is part of Northwind Health's broader plan to make same-day care available to every patient it serves, whether in a clinic or online.

About Northwind Health

Northwind Health is an outpatient care organization headquartered in Minneapolis. Its roughly 2,300 employees deliver primary care, urgent care, pediatrics, women's health and behavioral health at 34 clinics and through the Northwind Anywhere telehealth service.
