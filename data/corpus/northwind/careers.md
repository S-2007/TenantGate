CAREERS AT NORTHWIND HEALTH
===========================

Last updated 2026-09-14

Northwind Health is hiring. We are a Minneapolis-based outpatient care and telehealth organization with about 2,300 colleagues across Minnesota, Wisconsin, and Iowa. If you want to practice medicine (or support people who do) without the overhead and politics of a giant hospital system, we would like to meet you.

Why people join us
------------------

- **Reasonable panels.** Our primary care physicians carry a target panel of about 1,800 patients, below the industry norm, and every physician is paired with a dedicated medical assistant.
- **Real flexibility.** Many clinicians split their week between in-person clinic days and Northwind Anywhere telehealth shifts.
- **Teams that stay.** Our nursing turnover in 2025 was 11%, and we are proud of that number.
- **Growth.** We have opened six clinics since 2023 and are expanding pediatric and behavioral health care.

How hiring works
----------------

We try hard not to waste your time. Our typical process:

1. **Apply online.** Every application is read by a human recruiter within five business days.
2. **Recruiter screen** - a 20-minute phone call about the role, schedule and pay range.
3. **Hiring manager interview** - usually by video.
4. **Team interview or shadow shift.** Clinical candidates spend a half day at the clinic; Digital Health candidates do a paired technical session (no take-home assignments, ever).
5. **Offer and references.**

Most candidates go from application to offer in about three to four weeks. Clinical roles also require license verification and credentialing, which can add two to six weeks before your start date. We will tell you up front which steps apply.

A note on pay transparency
--------------------------

All job postings include a pay range. Where you land in the range depends on experience, certifications and location, and we will explain how we arrived at your offer.
